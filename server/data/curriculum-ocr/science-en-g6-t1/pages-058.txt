6-2 The Path of Water Transportation of Water in Plants Chapter 6

Point! The Relationship between Plants and Water
If you water the base of a wilted plant, the plant recovers after a while. This is because water spreads to the whole body of the plant.
There are paths in the roots, stems, and leaves of plants to carry water.
Water is transported in the order of Roots → Stems → Leaves.

Warm Up Let's observe the path of water!
Let's use colored water to visualize where the water passes through.
1 Preparation:
•Plant •Colored water •Flask
•Cutter knife (used only by the teacher) •Absorbent cotton
•Magnifying glass or microscope
2 Experiment:
1 Dig up the plant with the soil carefully, and wash the roots with water.
2 Put colored water in the flask and soak the roots in it.
3 After a few hours, observe the color change in the plant parts.
4 Cut the roots, stems, and leaves with a cutter knife, and observe the cut surfaces.
3 Summary:
•The parts that turned red are the paths for the colored water.
