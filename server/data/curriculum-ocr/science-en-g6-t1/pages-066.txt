Chapter 7

Point! Circulation of Air and Water
Connection of living organisms through air
Animals use the oxygen released by plants to breathe, and plants use the carbon dioxide breathed out by animals for photosynthesis.

Connection of living organisms through water
Plants release part of water into the air through transpiration. This water vapor joins the water cycle. Water also evaporates from oceans, rivers, and lakes, rises into the atmosphere to from clouds, and falls as rain. Transpiration is an important part of the water cycle that keeps water moving between living organisms and the environment.
Our lives are constantly supported by the oxygen produced by plants and the water circulating around the Earth!
