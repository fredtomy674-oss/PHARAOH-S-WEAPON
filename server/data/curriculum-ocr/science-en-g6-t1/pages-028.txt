3-2 The function of saliva
Digestion Chapter 3

Point! The Function of Saliva
Saliva in the mouth has the function of breaking down starch contained in food (like rice or bread) and starch is changed into another substance that is easy for the body to digest by saliva.
A liquid that has the function of digesting food, like saliva, is called a digestive juice. Digestive juices are produced not only in the mouth, but also in the stomach and the small intestine.

Warm Up Let’s see if saliva changes starch!
Follow the steps of the experiment and predict the results.
1 Preparation:
• Dilute starch solution (cooked rice, can also be used) • Water
• Dilute iodine solution • Beaker • Dropper • Hot water
• Test tubes • Safety goggles
• Cotton piece moistened with water
• Cotton piece moistened with saliva
2 Experiment:
1 Put the dilute starch solution into two test tubes with a dropper.
2 Put a cotton piece soaked with saliva into one test tube. Put a cotton piece soaked with water into the other test tube.
3 Put hot water of about 40 degrees Celsius in a beaker, and put the two test tubes in it for a few minutes.
4 Add 1 or 2 drops of dilute iodine solution to the warmed test tubes and compare the color changes.
3 Summary:
From the results of the experiment, confirm that the starch is broken down due to the function of saliva.
The reason we put them in hot water of about 40 degrees Celsius is to make the same environment as inside the body.
