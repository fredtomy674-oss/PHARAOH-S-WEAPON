6-1 Transpiration Transportation of Water in Plants Chapter 6

Point! Plants and Water
Some of the water absorbed from the roots goes out into the air from the leaves. The process of water inside the plant changing into water vapor and being released into the air is called transpiration.

Stomata:
These are small pores mostly found on the lower surface of the leaves. They act as exits for water vapor. Transpiration mainly happens through the stomata.
