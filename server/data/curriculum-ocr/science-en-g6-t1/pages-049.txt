Chapter 5 Photosynthesis 5-1 Sunlight and Starch

Warm Up Let's check how leaves produce nutrients!
Let's check the relationship between sunlight and starch in leaves.

1 Preparation:
• Plant
• Ethanol
• Hot water (about 80 degrees Celsius)
• Aluminum foil
• Beaker
• Dilute iodine solution
• Petri dish

2 The day before the experiment:
• Make small cuts in each leaf so you can tell A, B, and C apart.
• Cover the plant's leaves with aluminum foil so they do not receive sunlight.

3 Morning of the experiment day:
• Remove the aluminum foil from B.
• Take leaf A and check if there is any starch.

4 5 hours later:
• Take leaves B and C and check if there is any starch.

Check the starch in the leaf.
Remove the aluminum foil.
Check the starch in the leaf.
