Point! Discharging Waste Products
Waste products (unnecessary things produced in the body) are carried to the kidneys by the blood. There are two kidneys on the lower back on both sides of the backbone.

In the kidneys, waste products produced in the body and excess water are filtered out from the blood to become urine. The urine produced by the work of the kidneys is temporarily stored in the bladder, and then discharged out of the body.
