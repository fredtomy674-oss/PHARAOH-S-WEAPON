2 Diagnosing the Earth and the Nile
In modern Egypt, testing the properties of aqueous solutions is used as an important tool to examine soil and Nile River water to ensure their quality. The color change of litmus paper is considered an indicator of the solution's acidic or basic properties. This helps scientists identify the characteristics of these solutions and take the necessary measures to protect the environment and preserve its natural resources.

• The Health of the Soil for Growing Crops:
In agricultural fields in Egypt, farmers are keen to check whether the soil is acidic or alkaline. By adjusting soil properties to suit the crops they cultivate, such as cotton and wheat, they can improve plant growth and increase crop yield and quality.

• Water Quality Management of the Nile River:
The quality of the Nile River water, our water of life, is also scientifically monitored. Scientists carefully monitor wastewater discharged from factories to ensure it is not strongly acidic or alkaline, using science to protect a beautiful environment where fish and birds can live safely.
