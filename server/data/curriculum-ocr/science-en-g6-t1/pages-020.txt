Point! Breathing and How it Works
People inhale and exhale to obtain the oxygen from the air and get rid of carbon dioxide.
Taking in oxygen and letting out carbon dioxide is called breathing.
The process by which the body obtains oxygen and expels carbon dioxide is called respiration.

Warm Up Let's check what is in the air we breathe out!
Let's conduct an experiment to find out what is contained in exhaled air.

1 Preparation:
•Plastic bags (Polyethylene bags)
•Gas detector tubes (for carbon dioxide / for oxygen)
•Gas sampling pump
•Scissors
•clear Limewater
•Safety goggles

2 Test with clear limewater:
1 Prepare two plastic bags.
2 Breathe into one bag, and fill the other bag with room air.
3 Pour a little limewater into each bag and shake them well.
→ What can you conclude from how the limewater changes?

3 Checking the amount of gases in exhaled air:
Gas detector tubes can be used to measure the amounts of oxygen and carbon dioxide in the exhaled air.

exhaled air contains oxygen, but it contains a smaller amount of it compared to inhaled air. Also exhaled air contains a larger amount of carbon dioxide compared to inhaled air.
