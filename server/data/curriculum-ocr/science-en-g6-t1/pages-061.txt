Enrichment Corner Chapter 6 Column:
Blessings of the Nile-Plant Survival Strategies

1 The “Green Factory” That Turns Sunlight into energy
• Solar Panels on the Water Lotus:
The leaves of the lotus (water lily) floating on the Nile River have a flat and large shape. This shape helps them catch as much of Egypt's strong sunlight as possible so they can produce nutrients (sugar / starch) efficiently.

• Exchange of Gases:
When plants do photosynthesis, they take in the carbon dioxide that humans breathe out and release oxygen. The beautiful green plants along the Nile River act as a “natural air purifier” to keep the air in Egypt clean.
