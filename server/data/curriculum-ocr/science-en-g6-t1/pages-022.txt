Point! The lungs are the site of gas exchange
The air enters from the nose and mouth then passes through the trachea to reach the left and right lungs in the chest.
Gas exchange
• Through the blood vessels around the lungs, oxygen in the air is taken into the blood, and carbon dioxide in the blood is let out into the air.
• Blood transports oxygen to the whole body by the work of the heart.
• Air with a lot of carbon dioxide passes through the trachea and is breathed out from the nose and mouth.
