Point! Blood Circulation and How the Heart Works

Nutrients absorbed from the small intestine and oxygen taken in by the lungs are carried to the whole body by the blood. The blood is pumped out by the heart, travels throughout the body, and returns to the heart again.

The movement of the heart during pumping out blood is called a heartbeat.
The heartbeat travels through the blood vessels and can be felt as a pulse at the wrist.

Blood Circulation
• Blood flows through blood vessels that spread all over the body, and circulates inside the body.
• The blood pumped out from the heart carries oxygen and nutrients to the whole body, collects carbon dioxide and waste produced in the body, and returns to the heart again.
• The blood that has returned to the heart is sent to the lungs to let out carbon dioxide and take in oxygen.
