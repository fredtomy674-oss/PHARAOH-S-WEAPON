1 The Discovery of Fire and Egyptian Civilization
Learning to use fire was a major step
for human civilization.

•Lighting in Ancient Times:
The Ancient Egyptians burned
vegetable oil extracted from plants
grown on the banks of the Nile River,
as well as animal fats, to illuminate
homes, temples, and pyramids. They
used clever ideas to minimize smoke,
demonstrating their wisdom regarding
the reduction of air pollution.

•The Role of Oxygen in Combustion:
When there is plenty of oxygen,
things burn intensely. Ancient
craftspeople used a bellows
(a tool for blowing air) to
pump air into the fire to supply
more oxygen, melting gold at
high temperatures to make
beautiful jewelry.
