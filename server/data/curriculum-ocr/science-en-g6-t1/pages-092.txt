2 How to Use a Gas Detector Tube
1 Snap off both ends:
Use the special tip holder to snap off both ends of the detector tube.
Insert detector tube. Rotate to score tube tip. Snap off the tip.
Tip Holder

2 Check the arrow and attach the tube:
Push the detector tube firmly into the opening of the gas sampler so the arrow (→) points toward the sampler.
[Caution!]
• Always put on the rubber cap so you do not get hurt by the broken glass edges.
Rubber Cap Gas Detector Tube Sampler

3 Draw in the gas and wait:
Pull the handle all the way out at once and lock it in place. Wait for the required time (about 1 minute).
Do not remove the tip of the gas detector tube from the bag for about 1 minute.
Leave it as it is for about 1 minute.
Polybag containing gas
With the mark positions aligned, pull the handle all the way out and lock it in place.

4 Read the scale:
Read the scale where the color has changed.
[Caution!]
• Oxygen detector tubes get hot, so do not touch them right away.
• Throw away used detector tubes exactly as your teacher tells you.
