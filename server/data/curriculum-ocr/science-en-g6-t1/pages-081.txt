1 The Secret of Solutions and the Pharaoh's Wisdom
Water solutions may look transparent and similar, but they differ in their properties — being acidic, neutral, or alkaline — that can be distinguished using litmus paper. The wisdom to control these properties has been in Egypt for thousands of years, cleverly used as a magic powder called natron to help make mummies to help preserve bodies.

• Natural Alkaline Powder "Natron":
Ancient Egyptians used a white powder called natron, found in desert salt lakes, which was strongly alkaline. It had a powerful ability to absorb water from the body, making it an essential material for creating mummies that kept their shape for thousands of years.

• Acids and Alkalis in Our Daily Lives:
Even today, we use the power of these properties without realizing it, such as lemon juice (acidic) for cooking and soapy water (alkaline) for cleaning.
