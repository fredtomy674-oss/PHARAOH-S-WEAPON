2 Water Paths and the Wisdom of Papyrus
• Papyrus plant:
Papyrus, a plant used to make paper in ancient times, grows many meters tall near the water. Inside its stem, there are strong, straight water paths that carry the water absorbed from the roots all the way to the top at once.

• Plants act as a Natural Water Sprinkler:
Transpiration is the process where water vapor comes out of the plant leaves, and it works to lower the temperature around the plant. Egypt is a country with a desert climate; therefore, plants contribute to cooling the surrounding atmosphere through the process of transpiration, acting as a natural air conditioner that helps cool the air and make it more moderate nearby.
