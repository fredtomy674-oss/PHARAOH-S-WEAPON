5-1 Sunlight and Starch Photosynthesis Chapter 5

Point! Plants and Nutrients
When sunlight falls on the leaves, the plant performs photosynthesis; it uses water and carbon dioxide gas to make its food (sugar), and stores part of it in the form of starch needed for growth.

How to check if sunlight is needed to produce starch
• Condition to change: Whether the leaf gets sunlight or not.
• Since there might already be starch in the leaves, cover the leaves from the day before the experiment so that sunlight does not fall on them.

Reaction with iodine solution
When you add iodine solution to starch, the color changes to dark blue.

How to check for starch in leaves
1 Put the leaf in hot water to make it soft.
[Caution!]
• Do not touch the hot water directly, or you will get burned.
2 Put the leaf from 1 into ethanol warmed in hot water. (To remove the green color from the leaf)
[Caution!]
• Ethanol is highly flammable, so do not heat it directly over a flame.
3 Wash the leaf from 2 with hot water, and dip it in dilute iodine solution.
