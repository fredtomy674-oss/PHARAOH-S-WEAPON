1-3 Air After Burning Conditions for Burning Chapter 1

Point! Changes in Air Composition After Burning
When things burn, a part of the oxygen in the air is used, and carbon dioxide is produced.
•Oxygen:
The amount decreases because it is used when things burn.
•Carbon dioxide:
The amount increases because it is newly produced when things burn.
Limewater
•We use clear limewater to check if there is carbon dioxide.
•Colorless and clear limewater has a property of turning milky white when it reacts with carbon dioxide.

Warm Up Let's check the change in air with limewater!
Let's find out what has increased in the air after burning.
