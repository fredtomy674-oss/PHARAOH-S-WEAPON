Point! How the Food Chain Works
The relationship between eater-and-eaten (the feeding among living organisms) is called a food chain.
1 Plants: They use sunlight to produce nutrients inside their bodies. This is the beginning of the food chain.
2 Herbivores: Animals that eat plants.
(Example: Grasshoppers, rabbits, etc.)
3 Carnivores: Animals that eat other animals.
(Example: Snakes, falcons, eagles, lions, etc.)
The connection is shown using arrows like "Grass → Grasshopper → Frog → Snake → Owl". The arrow points to the direction in which energy is moved.

Warm Up Let's complete the connection!
Imagine the connections between living organisms around you and connect the chain.
1 Find the start:
First, find a plant in your garden or a park.
2 Find what eats it:
What kind of bugs are eating the leaves of that plant?
3 Think further ahead:
Are there any birds or lizards that eat that insect?
4 Draw arrows:
Connect the living organisms you observed with arrows, and write down one food chain in your notebook.
