Point! Digestion and How it Works
Breaking down food with teeth and changing it into a form that is easy to absorb is called digestion.
Food that enters from the mouth is carried in the order of Mouth → Pharynx → Esophagus → Stomach → Small intestine →
Large intestine, and indigested food comes out from the anus. The path of food from the mouth to the anus is called the digestive tract (canal).
