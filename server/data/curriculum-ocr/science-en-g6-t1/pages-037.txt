Warm Up Let's measure your pulse!
Let's check how our pulse changes when we exercise.

•Stopwatch

2 Experiment:
1 Put your finger near the base of your thumb on your wrist and find the place where it is beating (throbbing).
2 While sitting, count how many times your pulse beats in 1 minute.
3 After jumping lightly, measure it again.
