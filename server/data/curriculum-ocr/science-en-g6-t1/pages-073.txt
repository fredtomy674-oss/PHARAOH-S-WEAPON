Enrichment Corner Chapter 8 It Always Starts with "Plants"

1 The Base of the Pyramid:
The diagram of a food chain is called a food pyramid, and the very bottom of this pyramid is always supported by plants (producers), which produce their own food from sunlight.

• The Nile's Chain:
In the Nile River, there is a beautiful circle of life: small shrimp eat water plants, fish (like the Nile tilapia) eat the shrimp, and then water birds and man eat the fish.

Nile River Food Pyramid
Water Birds & Humans (Apex Predators)
Nile Tilapia & Small Fish (Secondary Consumers)
Small Shrimp & Aquatic Insects (Primary Consumers)
Water Plants & Algae (Producers)
