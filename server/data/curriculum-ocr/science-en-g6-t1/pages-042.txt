Point! Arrangement and Teamwork of Organs
In our body, many organs are lined up closely without gaps, and they work together each other.
Organs in the chest: There are left and right lungs, and the heart sandwiched between them. They are protected by the ribs.
Organs in the abdomen:
There is the stomach at the top, the long small intestine below it, and the large intestine around it.
Organs in the lower back on the back side:
There is one kidney on each side of the backbone.
All organs are connected by blood vessels, and the whole body works by the blood carrying nutrients and oxygen and collecting waste products.
