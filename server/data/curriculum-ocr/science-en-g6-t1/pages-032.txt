Point! The Structure of the Small Intestine and Absorption of Nutrients
The nutrients from digested food are mainly absorbed in the small intestine along with water. After that, more water is absorbed in the large intestine, and what is left is eliminated from the anus as feces.
Structure of the small intestine
•Villi:
Small bumps on the inside of the small intestine. Because villi are lined up closely, the inner surface area of the small intestine becomes larger, and it can absorb nutrients and water efficiently.
Villi
Blood capillaries
The nutrients absorbed in the small intestine are carried to the whole body by the blood, passing through the liver. The liver works to store some of the nutrients and send them out to the whole body when needed.
