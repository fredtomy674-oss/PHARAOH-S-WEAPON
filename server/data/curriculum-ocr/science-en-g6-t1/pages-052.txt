Point! The Relationship between Plants and Air
! When sunlight is hitting the leaves, plants take in carbon dioxide and release oxygen.
! Plants also breathe to live and grow. In breathing, they take in oxygen and release carbon dioxide.
! The relationship between day, night, and air
• Daytime: Because there is light, taking in carbon dioxide and releasing oxygen is very active.
* Breathing is also happening, but overall, they take in more carbon dioxide.
• Nighttime: Because there is no light, they only breathe, taking in oxygen and releasing carbon dioxide.

Warm Up How does the plant change the air inside the bag!
Let's predict how the air will change after a while when a plant is wrapped in a bag.
1 Preparation:
• Plant • Polyethylene bag • Straw • Twist tie • Scissors
2 Experiment:
1 On a sunny day, put a bag over the plant, tie the base with a twist tie, and breathe into it.
2 Make a small hole in the bag with scissors.
3 Leave it in a place with sunlight for about 1 day.
4 Predict how the air inside the bag changed after this period.
3 Checking the percentage of both carbon dioxide gas and oxygen gas:
• Let's use the gas detector tube to check how the percentage of carbon dioxide and oxygen has changed.
In sunlight: The amount of carbon dioxide inside the bag decreases, and the amount of oxygen increases.
