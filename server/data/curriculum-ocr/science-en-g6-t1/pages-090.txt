2 The Recycling to Reuse Resources
Substances may change from one form to another during chemical reactions, but they do not disappear. Understanding these transformations helps us reuse materials and benefit from them again; therefore, modern industries and recycling processes rely on this knowledge to conserve Earth's resources and minimize waste.

• Evaporation Technique for Extracting Materials:
The evaporation technique is used to separate dissolved solid materials from solutions, where the water evaporates leaving behind the solid solute. This technique is used in some industries to extract high-purity materials.

• Egypt's Future and Recycling:
Old metal tools are not considered useless waste; rather, they can be recycled and transformed into new materials and products. Understanding chemical changes helps us make better use of resources, which reduces waste and supports a more sustainable future for Egypt.

Even if a metal reacts and becomes invisible, its original parts are still hidden inside. How to take them out and reuse them — that is where the future scientists will show their skills!
