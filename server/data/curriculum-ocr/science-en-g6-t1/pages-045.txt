The Mystery of the Human Body-Ancient Wisdom and Modern Science

1 What Mummies Taught Us About the Locations of Our Organs
Ancient Egyptians were the first people
in the world to know the exact locations
and shapes of body organs, thanks to
their mummy-making skills.

• The Secret of Canopic Jars:
When ancient Egyptians made
mummies, they kept four very important organs — the lungs, stomach, liver,
and intestines — safe in special jars. They knew that these were important
"engines" that supported human life.

• The Heart is the "Place of Wisdom":
The heart is a pump that sends blood throughout the body. However,
ancient people believed the heart was where the "mind" resided, so they
left it inside the body.

Canopic Jars
• Imsety: Has a human shape and was believed to be the god who protects
the liver.
• Hapi: Has a baboon shape and was believed to be the god who protects
the lungs.
• Duamutef: Has a jackal (wild dog) shape and was believed to be the god
who protects the stomach.
• Qebehsenuef: Has a falcon shape and was believed to be the god who
protects the intestines.
