What kind of tools are there to check gases?
1 Gas Detector Tube
• Inside the glass tube, there is a chemical substance that changes color when it reacts to a specific gas.
• By reading the scale where the color has changed, you can measure the amount of the gas.
• The type of tube to use is different for oxygen and carbon dioxide.
How to use
1 Ask your teacher to break off both ends of the glass tube, then insert it firmly into the gas sampling pump.
2 Pull the handle of the pump fully, and wait for a certain period of time. Then, the chemical substance inside reacts and changes color.
3 Read the number at the border where the color changed. This is the percentage of that gas included in the air.
[Caution!]
• The glass tube is single-use only. Also, be careful not to get hurt by the broken ends.

2 Gas Monitor
• It uses a sensor to show the percentage (%) of the gas on the screen in digital numbers.
• There are types that can measure both oxygen and carbon dioxide at the same time, which is very useful.
