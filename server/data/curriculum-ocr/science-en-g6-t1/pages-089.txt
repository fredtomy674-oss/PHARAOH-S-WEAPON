Enrichment Corner Chapter 10 The Science of Change-How Metals are Reborn

1 The Mystery of Gold That Shines Forever
The chemical reaction in which some metals react with acids, dissolving and releasing gas, may help us understand the differences in properties between materials. By studying these reactions, we can explain one of the secrets of ancient Egyptian civilization: how the Pharaoh's golden mask maintained its luster and shine despite the passage of thousands of years.

• Why the Pharaoh Chose Gold:
Tutankhamun's golden mask retaining its beauty and luster to this day is due to gold being a highly stable metal. It does not react easily with surrounding substances, nor does it dissolve in strong solutions such as hydrochloric acid; therefore, it has maintained its shiny appearance despite the passage of thousands of years.

• The Energy Released When Metals React:
The bubbles that form when iron or aluminum reacts with dilute hydrochloric acid are actually hydrogen gas. During this chemical reaction, the reactants are transformed into new substances, and this may be accompanied by the release of a certain amount of energy.
