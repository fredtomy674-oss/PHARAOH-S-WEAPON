2 Surviving in the Harsh Desert
The water flowing in Egypt is actually part of a "global cycle" that reaches all the way from countries far to the south.

• The Desert Hunter Fennec Fox:
In the desert where there are few plants, animals that eat the small amount of grass seeds or lizards sustain the food chain. Without consumers like the fennec fox, the balance of the desert ecosystem would be disrupted.

• Memories of Wadi al-Hitan:
Wadi al-Hitan, or the "Valley of the Whales," is a desert now, but it is filled with fossils from when it used to be an ocean. By studying this place, we can see that tens of millions of years ago, the ancient ocean had a complete food chain just like the Nile does today. From the past to the future, the connection of life remains unbroken.
