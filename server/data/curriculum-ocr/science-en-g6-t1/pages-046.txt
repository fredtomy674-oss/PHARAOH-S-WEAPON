2 Blood Circulation: The Connecting Link Between Body Organs
In modern science, we learn that our organs do not work separately. They are all connected together by the circulation of blood.

SYSTEMIC CIRCULATION
PULMONARY CIRCULATION
TO ENTIRE BODY

BRAIN: Gives commands, requires many nutrients and oxygen
LUNG: Takes in oxygen, lets out carbon dioxide
STOMACH & INTESTINE: Digests and absorbs food, passes nutrients to blood
HEART: A pump to send blood
LIVER: Stores nutrients, breaks down harmful substances
KIDNEY: Filters waste and extra water from blood to make urine

TYPES OF BLOOD
RED: Oxygen & nutrient-rich blood
BLUE: Carbon dioxide & waste-rich blood

FILTRATION IN KIDNEY

• The Transporters of Oxygen and Nutrients:
The oxygen we breathe in through our lungs and the nutrients we absorb from our small intestine are all carried to cells all over our body by the blood, using the pumping power of the heart.

• Egyptian Food for Good Health:
Our bodies are made from the food we eat. A traditional Egyptian food called "Molokhia" is full of nutrients that help keep our blood flowing smoothly and help us digest our food. Learning how our body works helps us understand why taking care of our daily meals is so important.

Inside our bodies, the heart works without resting to pump blood and deliver oxygen and nutrients from our food to every corner of our body!
