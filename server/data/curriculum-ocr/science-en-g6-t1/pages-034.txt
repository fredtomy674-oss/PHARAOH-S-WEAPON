Let's take a closer look at the liver.
Functions of the Liver
• It converts some nutrients into a form that is easy for the body to use, stores them as energy, and sends them out to the whole body when needed.
• It breaks down harmful substances produced in the body so they can be eliminated in urine.
• It produces a digestive juice that helps break down and absorb fat.
