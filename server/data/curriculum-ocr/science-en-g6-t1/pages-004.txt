Introduction

Dear Student,
We are pleased to present this Science book to you as your companion on an exciting journey to explore the world around you and understand natural phenomena and everyday life through observation, investigation, and critical thinking.

This book has been designed in accordance with modern educational approaches that emphasize learning science by gradually building scientific knowledge. You will begin with facts, observations, and hands-on experiences, then progress toward scientific concepts and principles. Therefore, you will find a variety of activities, questions, and learning situations that encourage you to observe, classify, compare, collect data, and draw conclusions independently under your teacher's guidance.

The book begins with objects and phenomena that are familiar to your daily life, then gradually moves from the concrete to the abstract and from the simple to the more complex. Its carefully sequenced and connected scientific content integrates different fields of science to help you build a solid and meaningful scientific understanding.

The book also highlights the importance of inquiry-based learning and hands-on investigation while taking individual differences into account. It connects science with everyday life, environmental issues, society, health, and safety. Through a wide range of engaging activities, it develops your logical, critical, and creative thinking skills while encouraging cooperation, communication, and respect for others' opinions.

This book aims to develop a positive scientific mindset based on curiosity, accuracy, scientific integrity, and perseverance. It also helps you appreciate the role of science and scientists in improving human life while promoting the values of sustainable development through conserving natural resources, energy, and water, and protecting living organisms and the environment.

Every effort has been made to ensure that the language of this book is clear and easy to understand, and that its illustrations and activities are attractive and motivating. We hope that studying science will become an enjoyable and meaningful experience that inspires you to love science and prepares you to contribute positively to building a better future.

We wish you every success.
