Lab Safety and Professional Tools

In experiments, we handle fire sources and strong chemicals (like hydrochloric acid). This is a guide to help you do experiments safely and get the correct results.

1 Handling Chemicals
• Wear safety goggles:
Always make sure to wear safety goggles to protect your eyes from chemical splashes or spills, especially acids, such as hydrochloric acid.

• How to smell chemicals:
Use the hand-wafting method (Wafting):
When testing the odor of a chemical substance, do not bring the container directly close to your nose.
Instead, gently wave your hand over the opening of the container to safely direct a small amount of vapors toward your nose..

• If you spill a chemical:
In case of a chemical coming into contact with the skin, immediately rinse the affected area with a generous amount of running water, then inform your teacher or supervisor immediately.

• Always remember:
Following safety instructions and maintaining a clean and organized workspace help conduct experiments successfully and safely
