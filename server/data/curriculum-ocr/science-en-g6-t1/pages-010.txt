1-2 The Role of Oxygen Conditions for Burning Chapter 1

Point! Gases That Help Things Burn
Air is a mixture of gases like nitrogen, oxygen, and carbon dioxide.
Components of Air
Nitrogen (about 78%)
Oxygen (about 21%)
Carbon dioxide (about 0.04%), other gases.
Oxygen helps in burning, but nitrogen and carbon dioxide do not.

Warm Up Let's see the differences in burning caused by different gases!
Let's investigate which gas has the power to help things burn.
1 Preparation:
• Candle
• lighter (used by the teacher only)
• Nitrogen gas cylinder
• Carbon dioxide gas cylinder
• 3 lids
• Combustion spoon
• 3 glass jars
• Oxygen gas cylinder
• Water tank filled with water
• Bendy straw

2 Experiment:
1 Put nitrogen, oxygen, and carbon dioxide into the 3 jars, respectively.
How to collect the gas
Turn the water-filled jar upside down in the water, fill 70% of it with the gas, and put the lid on.
2 Stand a candle on the combustion spoon, then ask your teacher to light it, quickly put it into the jar, and put the lid on.
3 Observe how the candle's flame burns in each jar.

3 Summary:
• Summarize the state of the candle and flame in each of the three jars.
