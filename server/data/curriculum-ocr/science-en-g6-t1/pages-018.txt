•Energy Without Burning:
Instead of burning oil and coal, Egypt is now gaining attention from around the world for using "renewable energy" from the sun and wind.

•Benban Solar power plant:
The Benban Solar power plant in Aswan is considered one of the largest solar energy projects in the world. It generates electricity using sunlight without burning fuel, thereby reducing carbon dioxide emissions.

From the wisdom of mastering fire, to the wisdom of generating energy without it. The progress of science will save the future of our Earth!
