Point! Ecosystems in Egypt
Egypt has completely different food chains in aquatic environments (the Nile River) and arid areas (deserts).
Food Chain in the Nile River
Algae and water plants ➔ Herbivorous fish (like the Nile tilapia) ➔ Carnivorous fish (like the Nile perch) ➔ Top carnivores (like the Nile crocodile)
Food Chain in the Desert
Desert shrubs and grass ➔ Herbivores (like grasshoppers and jerboas) ➔ Top carnivores (like fennec foxes and Egyptian vultures)

Even if the types of animals are different, the chain always starts with plants and ends with large carnivores.
