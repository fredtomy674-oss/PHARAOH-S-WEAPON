THEME 1: WHO AM I?

THE GOLDEN EAGLE

Eagles need food, water, air,
and shelter.

Eagles eat meat from small
animals.

Eagles live in nests.

Eagles have holes for ears.

The holes are behind the
feathers.

Eagles have two feet.

THE GAZELLE
Gazelles need food, water, air,
and shelter.

Gazelles eat plants.

Gazelles live in grasslands.

Gazelles have ears and long
horns.

Gazelles have feet called
hooves.

Gazelles can jump high.
