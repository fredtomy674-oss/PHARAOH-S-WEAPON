It is clear from the above that the force that pulls (attracting) all objects downward towards the
center of the Earth (Figure 4) is called the gravitational force.
In general, forces are either contact forces that act on objects when they touch each
other, such as collision forces, elasticity forces and friction forces or field forces that act
over a certain distance, such as gravitational forces, electrostatic forces and magnetic forces.
