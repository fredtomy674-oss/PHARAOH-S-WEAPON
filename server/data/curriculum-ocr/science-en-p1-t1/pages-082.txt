Life Application
Some mothers, during making pickled olives (Figure 6), add a spoonful of sugar to the salt solution used to reduce the bitterness of the olives and improve their taste.
The scientific explanation for this is that the sugar acts as a food source for beneficial bacteria, which convert the sugar into lactic acid.

1 Penicillium Notatum
In 1928, the English scientist Alexander Fleming discovered that Penicillium Notatum (Figure 8) secretes a substance that stops the growth and reproduction of a certain type of bacteria. This substance was extracted and became known as penicillin, the well known antibiotic, which is used to kill the bacteria that cause some diseases like diphtheria and tonsillitis.
