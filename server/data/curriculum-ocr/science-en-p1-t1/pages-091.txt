The Cycle of The Seasons
The tilt of Earth's axis and its orbit around the Sun cause sunlight to fall on Earth's surface
at different angles, leading to variations in the amount of sunlight received by
Earth's hemispheres (the two halves of Earth's sphere) throughout the year.
This results in the alternation of the four seasons across 365 1/4 days.
