Combination with History
During Christopher Columbus's fourth voyage to the Americas in 1504, the native people of
Jamaica refused to continue supplying him with food. Columbus, knowing the date of
an upcoming lunar eclipse, threatened the leaders that the gods' wrath would descend upon
them if they continued to refuse his requests. When the eclipse occurred, the leaders believed
his story of the gods' anger and obeyed his orders.

Issue for Discussion
