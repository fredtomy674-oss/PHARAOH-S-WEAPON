Lesson Objectives :
By the end of the lesson, the student should be able to :
1 Conclude the similarities and differences among the planets in
the solar system.
2 Recognize some of the consequences of Earth's axis tilt.
3 Conclude the relation between the apparent motion of the sun and
the lengths of shadows cast by objects.
4 Conclude the relation between the tilt of Earth's axis and
its orbiting around the sun with changing seasons.
5 Conclude the relation between the number of the daylight hours and
the four seasons.
