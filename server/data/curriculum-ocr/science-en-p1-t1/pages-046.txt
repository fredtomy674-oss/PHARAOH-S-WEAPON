A profile of the Scientist
Charles-Augustin de Coulomb

Figure (33)
A French physicist who
formulated a law describing
electric forces between charged
particles, known as Coulomb's
Law or the Inverse Square Law.
His studies and discoveries in
the late 18th century laid the
foundation for the development
of electromagnetic theory. In his
honor, the unit of electric charge
(Coulomb) is named after him
