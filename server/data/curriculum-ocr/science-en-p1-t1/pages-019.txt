3 The Modern Periodic Table
Some inadequacies in Moseley’s table prompted the scientists to try to modify it. The
elements were rearranged in a regular ascending order according to their atomic numbers and
the way to fill the energy sublevels with the electrons in the modern periodic table (Figure 2),
which consists of 7 horizontal periods, 18 vertical groups, and contains 118 elements.
