Lesson Objectives :
By the end of the lesson, the student should be able to :
1 Provide evidence that all living organisms are composed of cells as
the basic unit of structure and function.
2 Classify living organisms into prokaryotes and eukaryotes.
3 Classify living organisms into unicellular and multicellular organisms.
4 Compare between prokaryotes and eukaryotes.
5 Recognize the role of stem cells in the differentiation of tissues and
organs in multicellular organisms.
