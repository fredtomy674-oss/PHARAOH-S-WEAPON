A profile of the Scientist
Alexander Fleming
Fleming, a Scottish scientist, is famous for discovering
penicillin, extracted from Penicillium Notatum fungus, which
is considered the first effective antibiotic. He noticed
the formation of a green fungus on the surface of one of
the bacterial cultures in his laboratory when it was exposed
to air, this fungus killed the surrounding bacteria. He was
awarded the Nobel Prize in Medicine in 1945 for this discovery
