UNIT 4
The (Earth - Sun - Moon) System

The lessons
Lesson one : The Earth and the Solar System
Lesson two : Lunar Eclipse

Learning Outcomes :
By the end of this unit, the student should be able to:
1. Understand the (Earth-Sun-Moon) system.
2. Explain data collected from monitoring devices about some characteristics of the planets in the solar system
3. Conclude the similarities and differences among the planets in the solar system.
4. Conclude the relation between the tilt of the Earth's axis and its orbiting around the Sun with changing of seasons.
5. Explain the phenomenon of the lunar eclipse as one of the phases of the Moon's cycle.
6. Write a report on the causes of solar eclipse.
