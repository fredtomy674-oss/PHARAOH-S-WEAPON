Properties of Ionic and Covalent Compounds
Most ionic compounds dissolve in water; both their aqueous solutions and their moltens
conduct electricity; their melting and boiling points are high.
On the other hand, most covalent compounds do not dissolve in water and mostly do not
conduct electricity, and they have low melting and boiling points.
