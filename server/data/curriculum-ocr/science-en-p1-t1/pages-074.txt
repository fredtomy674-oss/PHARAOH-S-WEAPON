breaking down the organic nutrients, especially glucose,

3 Transport :
Eukaryotic organisms, such as human, have specialized transport systems that transport
digested food and oxygen extracted from the atmospheric air through the blood. The blood
carries them to the heart, that pumps them to all the cells of the body and then it returns to
the heart once again in a closed cycle described as the circulation.
