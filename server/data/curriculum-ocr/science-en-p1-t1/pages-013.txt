Life Application

Fertilizers are chemical compounds used to improve
the agricultural crops (Figure 7).
Among the most important types of fertilizers is the fertilizer NPK
which is composed of three compounds containing the elements :
• Nitrogen (N) : Necessary for the greening of the plant leaves.
• Phosphorus (P) : Necessary for developing strong roots.
• Potassium (K) : Necessary for the healthy growth of the plant.
