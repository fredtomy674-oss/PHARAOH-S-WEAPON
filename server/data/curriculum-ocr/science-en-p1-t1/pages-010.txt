Structure of The Atom
Many attempts were made to discover the structure of the atom. Ancient Greek philosophers believed that matter was composed of small, indivisible parts called atoms.
In the early 19th century, Dalton formulated the first scientific theory about the atom, showing that atoms are indivisible. Rutherford's model of atom (1909) was the first atomic model formulated on experimental basis.
Scientists discovered that there is a very small space in the atom that contains two types of particles: protons and neutrons. This space is called the nucleus, in which electrons revolve around this nucleus at very high speeds in certain energy levels.
Protons, neutrons and electrons are called subatomic particles.
