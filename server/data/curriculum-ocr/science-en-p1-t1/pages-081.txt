1 Beneficial Bacteria

1 Root Nodule bacteria and decomposition bacteria
Green plants need carbon, hydrogen and oxygen
elements to produce carbohydrates through
the photosynthesis process. They also need nitrogen
element to produce the proteins which are used in the
growth of their cells and tissues.
However, plants cannot use nitrogen in its gaseous
form from the air or soil, so types of beneficial microbes
in the soil provide the plant with nitrogen in a usable
form, such as root nodule bacteria (Figure 2), which live
on the roots of leguminous plants like clover, bean and
peas in specialized structures known as root nodules.

Life Application
Farmers often leave the roots of leguminous plants in the soil after harvesting the crops to be
decomposed by the decomposition bacteria into nitrogenous compounds soluble in water, which
increases soil fertility and maintains the cycle of the necessary elements in nature.
