When Earth is positioned between the moon and the sun in the middle of the Arabic (lunar)
month, the moon appears as a complete disc, and it is said to be in the Full Moon phase.
It is observed once or twice a year that the Full Moon gradually darkens until it completely
disappears in the same period of the lunar month, this phenomenon is known as
The Lunar eclipse.
