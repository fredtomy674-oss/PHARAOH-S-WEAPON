Shapes of Magnets
Lodestone, (Figure1), is a natural magnet rock that was first found in Magnesia (a regional unit) of ancient Greece, and its distinguishing characteristic is the ability to attract certain metallic objects. The production of the industrial magnets began in the 19th century. There are different shapes of magnets, some of which are shown in (Figure 2).
