Positive and negative measures of a directed angle
In figure (1) the directed angle, resulting from an anticlockwise rotation has a positive
measure.
In figure (2) the directed angle, resulting from a clockwise rotation has a negative measure.

Example
1 Find the measure of the directed angle θ in each of the following figure:
A B C D
θ 55° θ 33° θ 125° θ 134°

Solution
We know that the sum of measures of accumulative angles around a point equals 360°
A Direction of the angle θ is a clockwise direction m(∠θ) = – (360° – 55°) = – 305°
B Direction of the angle θ is an anticlockwise direction m(∠θ) = 360° – 33° = 327°
C Direction of the angle θ is an anticlockwise direction m(∠θ) = 360° – 125° = 235°
D Direction of the angle θ is a clockwise direction m(∠θ) = – (360° – 134°) = – 226°
