The Relation Between the Areas of two Similar Polygons

Example
1 In the figure opposite: ABC is a triangle D ∈ AB
where AD/DB = 3/4, DE // BC and intersects AC at E.
if the area of Δ ABC = 784cm². find:
A area of Δ ADE. B area of trapezium DBCE.

Solution
In ΔADC: ∵ DE // BC
∴ Δ ADE ~ Δ ABC (Corollary)
∴ a(Δ ADE) / a(Δ ABC) = (AD/AB)² (theorem)
and a(Δ ADE) / 784 = (3/7)² ∴ a(Δ ADE) = 784 × 9/49 = 144cm²
∵ area of trapezium DBCE = area of Δ ABC - area of Δ ADE
∴ area of trapezium DBCE = 784 - 144 = 640cm²

Example
2 The ratio of the areas of two similar triangles equals 4 : 9. If the perimeter of the greater triangle equals 90cm, Find the perimeter of the smaller triangle.

Solution
Let Δ ABC ~ Δ DEF
∴ a(Δ ABC) / a(Δ DEF) = (AB/DE)² = 4/9 AB/DE = 2/3
∵ Perimeter of Δ ABC / Perimeter of Δ DEF = AB/DE = 2/3
∴ Perimeter of (Δ ABC) / 90 = 2/3
∴ Perimeter of Δ ABC = 60cm
