The Relation Between the Areas of two Similar Polygons

Example

(5) The ratio of the perimeters of two similar polygons equals $3:4$, if the sum of their areas equals $225\text{cm}^2$, then find the area of each of them.

Solution

$\because$ The ratio of the perimeters of the two similar polygons $= 3 : 4$

$\because$ The ratio of the lengths of any two corresponding sides of them $= 3 : 4$

Let the area of the first polygon be $9x$,

and the area of the second polygon be $16x$

$\because 9x + 16x = 225$, then $x = \frac{225}{9 + 16} = 9$

$\because$ area of the first polygon $= 9 \times 9 = 81\text{cm}^2$

$\because$ area of the second polygon $= 16 \times 9 = 144\text{cm}^2$
