Similarity of Triangles
Postulate
AA similarity postulate
If two angles of one triangle are congruent to their two corresponding
angles of another triangle, then the two triangles are similar

Notice that
1- Any two equilateral triangles are similar
2- Two isosceles triangles are similar if the measure of one of the two base angles in one of
them is equal to the measure of one of the two base angles in the other . or if the measures
of their vertex angles are equal.
3- Two right triangles are similar if the measure of one of the two acute angles in one of
them is equal to the measure of one of the two acute angles in the other.
