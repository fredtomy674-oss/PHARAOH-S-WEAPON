Example

9 Convert $30^{\circ}$ to radian measure in terms of $\pi$.
To convert to radian measure we use the formula $\frac{x^{\circ}}{180^{\circ}} = \frac{\theta^{\text{rad}}}{\pi}$

$$\theta^{\text{rad}} = \frac{30^{\circ} \times \pi}{180^{\circ}} = \frac{\pi}{6}$$

Example

10 Convert $1.2^{\text{rad}}$ to the degree measure.

Solution

$$x^{\circ} = \frac{1.2 \times 180^{\circ}}{\pi}$$
$$x^{\circ} = 68.75493542 = 68^{\circ} 45' 18''$$

There is another unit for measuring angle which is (Grad) and it equals $\frac{1}{200}$ of the measure of the straight angle.
If $x$, $\theta$, $y$ are three measures of angles in degree, radian, and Grad respectively, then:
$\frac{x^{\circ}}{180^{\circ}} = \frac{\theta^{\text{rad}}}{\pi} = \frac{y^{\text{grad}}}{200}$
