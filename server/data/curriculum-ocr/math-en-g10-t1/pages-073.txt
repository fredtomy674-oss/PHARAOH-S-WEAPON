Example
8 Industry: Fertilizer packages produced from
one of the factories are transfered by sliding on
a tube that is inclined and carried on to trucks
to the centre of distributions as in the figure
opposite.
If D, E and F are the projections of the points A, B
and C on the horizontal respectively, AB = 1.2m,
DE = 80cm , EF = 12m. Find the length of the
tube to the nearest metre .
C B A F 12 m D 80 cm
Solution
∵ D, E and F are projections to the points A, B and C on the horizontal ∴ AD // BE // CF
∵ AD // BE // CF , AC , DF are two transversals to them ∴ AC/AB = DF/DE : AC/1.2 = (12 + 0.8) / 0.8
∴ AC = (1.2 × 12.8) / 0.8 = 19.2 m ∴ A C ≃ 19 metres
