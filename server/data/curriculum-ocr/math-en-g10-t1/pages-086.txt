You have known that the degree measure depends on dividing the circle into 360 equal arcs in length, then:
Standard Position
1- The central angle subtends one of these arcs, its measure equals one degree (1°)
Positive measure
2- Each degree is subdivided into 60 equal divisions , each division in called a minute and is denoted by (1')
Negative measure
3- Each minute is subdivided into 60 equal divisions, each division is called " a second" and is denoted by (1")
Equivalent Angle
Quadrantal Angle
i.e.: 1° = 60' and 1' = 60"
