Think and discuss
We know that the two roots of the equation 4x² - 8x + 3 = 0 are 1/2 and 3/2.
Sum of the two roots = 1/2 + 3/2 = 1+3 / 2 = 2
Product of the two roots = 1/2 × 3/2 = 3/4

Sum and product of two roots
The two roots of the quadratic equation ax² + bx + c = 0 are:
-b + √ b²-4ac / 2a , -b - √ b²- 4ac / 2a
let the first root be L and the other root be M then :
L + M = -b / a (Prove that) L M = c / a (Prove that)

Example
1 Without solving the equation, find the sum and the product of the
two roots of the equation: 2x² + 5x - 12 = 0
Solution
a = 2 , b = 5 , c = - 12
Sum of the two roots = -b / a = -5 / 2 = -5/2
product of the two roots = c / a = -12 / 2 = -6
