Applications of Similarity in the circle

Example
5 ABC is a triangle in which AB = 8cm, AC = 4cm, D ∈ AC, D ∉ AC where CD = 12cm.
prove that AB touches the circle which passes through the points B, C, and D

Solution
∴ AC × AD = 4 (4 + 12) = 64,
(AB)² = (8)² = 64
∴ (AB)² = AC × AD
∴ AB touches the circle which passes through the points
B, C, and D at the point B.
