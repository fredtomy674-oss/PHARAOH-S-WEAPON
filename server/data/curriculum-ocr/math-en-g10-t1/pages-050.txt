Given: Δ ABC ~ Δ DEF
R.t.p.: a(Δ ABC) / a(Δ DEF) = (AB/DE)² = (BC/EF)² = (CA/FD)²
Proof: Draw AX ⊥ BC where AX ∩ BC = {X},
DY ⊥ EF where DY ∩ EF = {Y}
∵ Δ ABC ~ Δ DEF
∴ m(∠B) = m(∠E), AB/DE = BC/EF = CA/FD (1)
In the two triangles ABX, DEY:
m(∠X) = m(∠Y) = 90°, m(∠B) = m(∠E)
∴ Δ ABX ~ Δ DEY (A A similarity postulate)
∴ AB/DE = AX/DY (2)
a(Δ ABC) / a(Δ DEF) = (1/2 BC × AX) / (1/2 EF × AY) = BC/EF × AX/DY
By substituting from (1), (2), we get:
a(Δ ABC) / a(Δ DEF) = AB/DE × AB/DE = (AB/DE)² = (BC/EF)² = (CA/FE)² Q.E.D.

Notice that: a(Δ ABC) / a(Δ DEF) = (AB/DE)², AB/DE = AX/DY
then: a(Δ ABC) / a(Δ DEF) = (AX/DY)²
i.e. the ratio of the areas of the surfaces of two similar triangles equals the square of the ratio the lengths of any two corresponding alttitudes of the two triangles.
