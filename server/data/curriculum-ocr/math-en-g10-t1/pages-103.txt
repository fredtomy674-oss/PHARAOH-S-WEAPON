Trigonometric Functions

Try to solve
2 If 90° < θ < 180°, sin θ = 4/5 find cos θ, tan θ where θ is an angle in the standard position
in a unit circle.

Example
4 If the angle θ is drawn in the standard position and its terminal side passes through the
point B (-3/5, 4/5).
then fine all trigonometric ratios of the angle θ.
Solution
sin θ = 4/5 , cos θ = -3/5 = -3/5 , tan θ = 4/-3 = -4/3
csc θ = 5/4 , sec θ = 5/-3 = -5/3 , cot θ = -3/4 = -3/4

Trigonometric ratios of some special angles
In the figure opposite: the unit circle intersected the two
axes at the points
A1(1, 0), A2(0, 1), A3(-1, 0), A4(0, -1).
and θ is the measure of the directed angle A O B in the
standard position and its terminal side OB intersects the
unit circle at B.
first: If θ = 0° or θ° = 360° at: B(1, 0)
then: cos 0° = cos 360° = 1 , sin 0° = sin 360° = 0,
tan 0° = tan 360° = 0
second: If θ° = 90° = π/2 at: B(0, 1)
then: cos 90° = 0 , sin 90° = 1 , tan = 1/0 (undefied)
third: If θ° = 180° = π at: B(-1, 0)
then: cos 180° = -1 , sin 180° = 0 , tan 180° = 0
