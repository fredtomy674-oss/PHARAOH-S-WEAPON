Systems of Measuring Angle

And the unit of measuring angles in the radian measure is the radian angle which is denoted by (1rad) and is read as one radian.
Radian angle
It is a central angle in a circle subtends an arc of length equals the radius of this circle.
1 = rad

Critical thinking: Is the measure of the central angle in a circle is proportional to the length of the opposite arc? Explain your answer.

Example
8 A circle of radius 8cm. Find to the nearest hundredth the length of the arc opposite to a central angle of measure 5/12 π.
Solution
Use the formula of the length of the arc: l = θrad × r
r = 8 cm , θrad = 5π/12 , l = 5π/12 × 8 ∴ l ≃ 10.47cm

Relation between degree measure and radian measure:
You have known that: measure of the central angle in a circle equals the measure of its arc.
i.e. The central angle of degree measure 360°, then the length of its arc equals 2 π r
In the unit circle
2π in a radian measure is equivalent to 360° in a degree measure.
i.e. πrad is equivalent to 180° , 1rad = 180°/π ≃ 57° 17' 45"
If there is an angle of radian measure θrad and its degree measure x° then:
x°/180° = θrad/π

If the radius of a circle equals the unity then the circle is called the unit circle.
