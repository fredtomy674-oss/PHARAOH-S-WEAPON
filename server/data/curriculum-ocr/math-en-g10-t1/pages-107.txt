Key - Terms
▸ Related Angles

1- Trigonometric functions of two supplementary angles $\theta$, $(180^{\circ}-\theta)$
In the figure opposite, $\text{B}'(x', y')$ is the
image of the point $\text{B}(x, y)$ by reflection in
the y-axis, then $x'=-x$, $y'=y$ thus:

$\sin(180^{\circ}-\theta) = \sin\theta, \csc(180^{\circ}-\theta) = \csc\theta$
$\cos(180^{\circ}-\theta) = -\cos\theta, \sec(180^{\circ}-\theta) = -\sec\theta$
$\tan(180^{\circ}-\theta) = -\tan\theta, \cot(180^{\circ}-\theta) = -\cot\theta$

Definition
The related angles: are angles that the difference or
the sum of their measures equals a whole number of
right angles.
