Similarity of Polygons
If polygon ABCD ~ polygon XYZL then:
A ∠A ≡ ∠X, ∠B ≡ ∠Y, ∠C ≡ ∠Z, ∠D ≡ ∠L A B C D
B AB/XY = BC/YZ = CD/ZL = DA/LX = K (similarity ratio), K ≠ 0 ↓ ↓ ↓ ↓
The scale factor of similarity of polygon ABCD to polygon XYZL X Y Z L
equals K, and scale factor of similarity of polygon XYZL to
polygon ABCD equals
1/K
