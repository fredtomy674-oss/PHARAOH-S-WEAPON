1 In the figure opposite: AB ∩ CD = {E},

Find the length EB

Solution
∴ EA/EB = 4/3 , ∴ EC = 4K , EB = 3K where K ≠ 0
∴ AB ∩ CD = {E} ∴ EA × EB = EC × ED (well known problem)
then : 4K × 3K = 9 × 4
12K² = 36
K² = 3
K = √3 , EB = 3√3 cm
