f(x) = x² - 5x - 6

Step (2): We study the sign of the function f where f(x) = x² - 5x - 6, and represent it on the number line by putting f(x) = 0
x² - 5x - 6 = 0
x = 6 or x = -1
∴ (x - 6)(x + 1) = 0

Step (3): We determine the intervals which satisfy the inequality x² - 5x - 6 > 0
