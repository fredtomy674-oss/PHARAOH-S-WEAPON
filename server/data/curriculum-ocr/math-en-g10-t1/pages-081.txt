Angle Bisectors and Proportional Parts

Try to solve
5 In each of the following figures, prove that: EF // BC

Special cases
1- In Δ ABC:
If D ∈ BC, where BD/DC = BA/AC
then: AD bisects ∠ BAC

If E ∈ BC, E ∉ BC, where BE/EC = BA/AC
then: AE bisects the exterior angle of Δ ABC
at A, and it is defined by the converse of the previous theorem

2- In the figure opposite:
BE, CE are bisectors of angles B and C
intersecting at the point E ∈ AD. What do you deduce?

Fact: The bisectors of angles of a triangle are concurrent.

Example
6 ABC is a triangle in which AB = 18cm, BC = 15cm, AC = 12cm, D ∈ BC, where BD = 9cm,
AE ⊥ AD and intersects BC at E. prove that AD bisects ∠ BAC, then find the length of CE.

Solution
In Δ ABC: AB/AC = 18/12 = 3/2
CD = BC - BD = 15 - 9 = 6cm
∴ BD/DC = 9/6 = 3/2
∴ BD/DC = AB/AC AD bisects ∠ BAC
∴ AE ⊥ AD and intersects BC at E
∴ AE bisects the exterior angle of Δ ABC at A then BE/EC = AB/AC
∴ BE = BC + CE ∴ 15 + CE / CE = 18/12, CE = 30cm
