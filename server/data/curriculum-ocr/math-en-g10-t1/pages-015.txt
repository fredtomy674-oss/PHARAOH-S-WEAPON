2 Prove that the two roots of the equation 2x² - 3 x + 2 = 0 are complex and not real, then use the general formula to find two roots roots.

Solution
a = 2, b = - 3 and c = 2
∴ the discriminant = b² - 4ac ∴ the discriminant = (- 3)² - 4 × 2 × 2 = 9 - 16 = - 7
∴ the discriminant is negative ∴ there are two complex roots and not real

The general formula: x = - b ± √ b² - 4ac / 2a
x = - (-3) ± √ 7i² / 2 × 2 = 3 ± √ 7 i / 4
The two roots of the equation are: 3/4 + √7/4 i and 3/4 - √7/4 i
