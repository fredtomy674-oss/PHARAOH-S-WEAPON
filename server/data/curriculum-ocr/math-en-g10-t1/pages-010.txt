If a and b are two real numbers, then the number z = a + bi, is called a complex number,
where a is called the real part of the complex numer z and b i is the imaginary part of the complex
number z.

If b = 0, then the number z = a is a pure real and if a = 0, then the number z = bi is a pure
imaginary where b ≠ 0

Example
2 Solve the equation 9x² + 125 = 61
Solution
The equation 9x² + 125 = 61
9x² + 125 - 125 = 61 - 125 add (- 125) to both sides of the equation
9x² = - 64 simplify
x² = - 64/9 Divide both sides by 9
x = ± √-64/9 by taking the square root of both sides
x = ± √64/9 i = ± 8/3 i Definition

Equality of two complex numbers
Two complex numbers are equal if and only if the two real parts are equal and the two imaginary
parts are equal.
if: a + bi = c + di then: a = c and b = d and vice versa.

Example
3 Find the values of x and y which satisfy the equation: 2x - y + (x - 2y)i = 5 + i where x and
y ∈ R and i² = -1
Solution
By equalizing the two real parts and the two imaginary parts then
2x - y = 5 , x - 2y = 1
Solve the equations we get x = 3 , y = 1
