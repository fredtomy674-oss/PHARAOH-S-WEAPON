5- trigonometric functions of angles of measures θ and (90° + θ)
From congruency of the two triangles B'CO, OCB
We get y' = x, x' = -y

thus, it is possible to deduce all trigonometric functions of angles θ and (90° + θ) as follows:
sin (90° + θ) = cos θ, csc (90° + θ) = sec θ
cos (90° + θ) = -sin θ, sec (90° + θ) = -csc θ
tan (90° + θ) = -cot θ, cot (90° + θ) = -tan θ

Example

Solution
∵ tan (90° + θ) = -cot θ
∵ csc (90° + θ) = sec θ
∴ tan (90° + θ) = -1/(2√2) = -√2/4
