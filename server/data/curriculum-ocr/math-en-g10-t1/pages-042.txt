Important corollaries

corollary
1
If a line is drawn parallel to one side of a triangle and intersects the other
two sides or the lines containing them, then the resulting triangle is similar
to the original triangle.

If DE // BC and intersects AB and AC at D, and E respectively, as in the three previous figures:
Then: Δ ADE ~ Δ ABC.

Example
2 In the figure opposite: ABC is a triangle, D ∈ AB, DE // BC
and intersects AC at E, DF // AC and intersects BC at F.
Prove that: Δ ADE ~ Δ DBF

Solution
∵ DE // BC ∴ Δ ADE ~ Δ ABC (1)
∵ DF // AC ∴ Δ DBF ~ Δ ABC (2)
from (1) and (2): Δ ADE ~ Δ DBF (Q.E.D)
