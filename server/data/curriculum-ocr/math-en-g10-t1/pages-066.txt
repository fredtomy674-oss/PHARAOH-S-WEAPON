Theorem 1
If a line is drawn parallel to one side of a triangle and intersects the other two sides, then it divides them into segments whose lengths are proportional.
(Proof is not required)

Given: ABC is a triangle, DE // BC
R.t.p: AD/DB = AE/EC
Proof: ∵ DE // BC
∴ Δ ABC ~ Δ ADE (similarity postulate)
then: AB/AD = AC/AE (1)
∵ D ∈ AB, E ∈ AC
∴ AB = AD + DB, AC = AE + EC (2)
from (1) and (2) we get:
(AD + DB)/AD = (AE + EC)/AE
then: AD/AD + DB/AD = AE/AE + EC/AE
