5 Prove without using the calculator that: sin 60° cos 30° - cos 60° sin 30° = sin² π/4
Solution
You know that sin 30° = 1/2 , cos 30° = √3/2 , sin 60° = √3/2 , cos 60° = 1/2
∴ L.H.S = √3/2 × √3/2 - 1/2 × 1/2 = 3/4 - 1/4 = 1/2 (1)
∴ π/4 = 45° , sin 45° = 1/√2
R.H.S = sin² π/4 = sin² 45° = (1/√2)² = 1/2 (2)
From (1) and (2) ∴ the two sides are equal.
