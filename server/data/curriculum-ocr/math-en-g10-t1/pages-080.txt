Example
5 In the figure opposite: AD is a median in Δ ABC
DX bisects ADB and intersects AB at X.
DY bisects ADC and intersects AC at Y.
Prove that : XY // BC.

Solution
In Δ ADB: ∵ DX bisects ADB ∴ AD/DB = AX/XB (1)
In Δ ADC: ∵ DY bisects ADC ∴ AD/DC = AY/YC (2)
In Δ ABC: ∵ AD is a median ∴ DB = DC (3)
from (1), (2) and (3) AX/XB = AY/YC then XY // BC.
