Unit objectives
By the end of the unit, the student should be able to:
* Revise what he / she has previously studied in preparatory stage on similarity.
* Recognize similarity of two polygons.
* Recognize the theorem (if the length of the corresponding sides..)
* Recognize the theorem : ( If the measure of an angle of a triangle ...)
* Recognize the theorem ; ( the ratio between the areas of the surfaces of two similar triangle ...).
* Recognize and deduce the fact: ( Any two similar polygons can be divided into...)
* Recognize the theorem ; ( the ratio between the areas of the surfaces of two similar polygons...)
* Recognize and deduce the well known problem ; (If the two lines containing the two chords in a circle are intersecting at a point then ... ) and vice versa and corollaries on it .
