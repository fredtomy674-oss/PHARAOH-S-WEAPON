First: Multiple choice:

1 The angle of measure 60° in the standard position is equivalent to the angle of measure:
A 120° B 240° C 300° D 420°

2 The angle of measure 31π/6 lies in the quadrant
A First B second C third D fourth

3 The angle of measure -9π/4 lies in the quadrant
A First B second C third D fourth

where n is the number of its sides, then the measure of the angle of a regular pentagon in
radian measure equals:
A π/3 B 7π/2 C 3π/5 D 2π/3

5 The angle of measure 7π/3 its degree measure equals
A 105° B 210° C 420° D 840°

6 If the degree measure of an angle is 64° 48', then its radian measure equals
A 0.18 rad B 0.36 rad C 0.18 π D 0.36 π

7 The arc length in a circle of diameter length 24 cm and opposite to a central angle of
measure 30° is
A 2π cm B 3π cm C 4π cm D 5π cm

8 The measure of the central angle in a circle of radius length 15 cm and opposite to an arc
length 5π cm equals
A 30° B 60° C 90° D 180°

9 If the measure of an angle of a triangle equals 75° and the measure of another angle
equals π/4, then the radian measure of the third angle equals
A π/6 B π/4 C π/3 D 5π/12
