Similarity of Triangles

∴ AB/AX = BC/XY = CA/YA
∴ AX = DE (Construction)
AB/DE = BC/XY = CA/YA (1)
AB/DE = BC/EF = CA/FD (given) (2)
from (1), (2) we deduce that: XY = EF, YA = FD
and Δ AXY ≡ Δ DEF (SSS congruency theorem)
∴ Δ DEF ~ Δ AXY
∴ Δ ABC ~ Δ AXY (Proved)
∴ Δ ABC ~ Δ DEF (Q.E.D.)

Example
5 In the figure opposite: B, Y and C are collinear. Prove that:
A Δ ABC ~ Δ XBY
B BC bisects ∠ ABX

Solution
A In the two triangles ABC and XBY, we have:
AB/XB = 12/9 = 4/3 , BC/BY = 18+6/18 = 4/3
AC/XY = 18/13.5 = 4/3
, AC/XY = BC/BY = AB/XB i.e. Corresponding sides are proportional
∴ Δ ABC ~ Δ XBY

B ∴ Δ ABC ~ Δ XBY ∴ m(∠ ABC) = m(∠ XBY)
i.e.: BC bisects ∠ ABX

6 In the figure opposite: AB ∩ CD = {E} where AE/CE = BE/DE, AC/CE = BD/DE prove that AC // BD

Solution
∴ AE/CE = BE/DE ∴ AE/BE = CE/DE (Properties of proportion) (1)
∴ AC/CE = BD/DE ∴ AC/BD = CE/DE (Properties of proportion) (2)
From (1), (2) we get: AE/BE = CE/DE = CA/DB
i.e. Δ AEC ~ Δ BED
∴ m(∠ ACE) = m(∠ BDE)
They are corresponding w.r. to the transversal CE
∴ AC // BD
