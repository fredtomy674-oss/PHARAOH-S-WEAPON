Similarity of Triangles

corollary
2
In any right triangle, the altitude to the hypotenuse separates the triangle
into two triangles which are similar to each other and to the original triangle

In the figure opposite: ABC is a right angled triangle at A, AD ⊥ BC
Δ DBA and Δ ABC
m (∠ ADB) = m (∠ CAB) = 90° and ∠ B is a common angle.
∴ Δ DBA ~ Δ ABC (postulate) (1)
Similarly Δ DAC ~ Δ ABC (2)
∴ If each one of two triangles is similar to a third triangle, then the two triangles are similar.
∴ Δ DBA ~ Δ DAC ~ Δ ABC

Example
3 ABC is a right angled triangle at A, AD ⊥ BC . Prove that DA is a mean proportional to
DB and DC.

Solution
Given: Δ ABC: m (∠A) = 90°, AD ⊥ BC.
R.T.P: prove that (DA)² = DB × DC.
Proof: In Δ ABC
∴ m (∠A) = 90°, AD ⊥ BC
∴ Δ DBA ~ Δ DAC (Corollary)
and DA/DC = DB/DA i.e. (DA)² = DB × DC
