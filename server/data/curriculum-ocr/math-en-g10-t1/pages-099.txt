Key - Terms
The triangles $\text{BAC}$, $\text{EFC}$ and $\text{DBC}$ are similar (why?)
From similarity, then: $\frac{\text{BA}}{\text{AC}} = \frac{\text{EF}}{\text{FC}} = \frac{\text{DB}}{\text{BC}} = \sin C$ (why?)
i.e.: the trigonometric ratio of an acute angle is constant and does not change except the angle itself is changed.
