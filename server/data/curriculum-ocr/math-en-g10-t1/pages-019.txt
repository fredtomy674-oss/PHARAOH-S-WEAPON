2 If the product of the two roots of the equation 2x² - 3x + k = 0 equals 1, find the value of k, then solve the equation.

Solution
product of the two roots = c/a ∴ k/2 = 1 ∴ k = 2
a = 2, b = - 3, c = 2
The general formula: x = - b ± √ b²-4ac / 2a
= 3 ± √ 9 - 16 / 4 = 3 ± √ 7i² / 4 = 3 ± √ 7 i / 4
The solution set of the equation is { 3/4 + √ 7/4 i , 3/4 - √ 7/4 i }

3 If (1 + i) is one of the roots of the equation x² - 2 x + a = 0 where a ∈ R , then find:
A The other root
B the value of a .

Solution
a = 1 , b = -2 , c = a
A ∵ 1 + i is one of the two roots of the equation
∴ the other root = 1 - i because the two roots are conjugate and their sum = 2

B ∵ Product of the roots = a
∴ (1 + i) (1 - i) = a
∴ 1 + 1 = a
