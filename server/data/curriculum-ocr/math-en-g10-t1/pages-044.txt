4 In the figure opposite: ABC is a right angled triangle at A,
AD ⊥ BC prove that:
A (AB)² = BC × BD
B (AC)² = CB × CD

Solution
In Δ ABC:
∵ m (∠A) = 90°, AD ⊥ BC
∴ Δ ABD ~ Δ CBA (corollary)
∴ AB/CB = BD/BA , (AB)² = BD × BC
, Δ ACD ~ Δ BCA (corollary)
∴ AC/BC = CD/CA , (AC)² = CB × CD
