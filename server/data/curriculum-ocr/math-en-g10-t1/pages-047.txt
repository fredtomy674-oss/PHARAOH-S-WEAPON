Similarity of Triangles

B From similarity also ∠ BDE ≡ ∠ BAC ∴ m(∠ BDE) = m(∠ BAC)
∴ ∠ BDE is an exterior angle of the quadrilateral ACDE
∴ the figure ACDE is a cyclic quadrilateral.

Example
8 ABC is a triangle D ∈ BC where (AC)² = CD × CB. Prove that: Δ ACD ~ Δ BCA
Solution
In the two triangles ABC, DAC, ∠ C is a common angle (1)
∴ (AC)² = CE × CB
∴ AC/CB = CD/AC (2)
from (1), (2) we get Δ ACD ~ Δ BCA (theorem)
