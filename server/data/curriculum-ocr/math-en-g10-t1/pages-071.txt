Parallel Lines and Proportional Parts
Solution
AB ⊥ BD, CD ⊥ BD ∴ AB // CD
∵ AC ∩ BD = {E} , AB // CD
∴ EA/AC = EB/BD then 60/AC = 45 / (45 + 105)
∴ AC = (60 × 150) / 45 = 200 metres.
