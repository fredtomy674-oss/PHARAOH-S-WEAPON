Learn!
Isosceles triangle
An isosceles triangle is a triangle with two congruent sides.
• The two congruent sides are called the legs of the triangle.
• The angle between them is called the vertex angle.
• The other two angles are called the base angles.

Theorem 5-1 The isosceles triangle theorem.
The base angles of an isosceles triangle are congruent.
In Δ ABC:
If AB ≅ AC,
then ∠ B ≅ ∠ C
