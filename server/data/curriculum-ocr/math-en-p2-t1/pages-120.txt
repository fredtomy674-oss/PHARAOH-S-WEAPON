3 The mode of a frequency distribution with intervals
You can estimate the mode of a frequency distribution with intervals by drawing the histogram and identifying the interval with the most frequency, it is called the modal interval, and then determine the projection of the intersection point of AB and CD on the horizontal axis, which is the mode.
