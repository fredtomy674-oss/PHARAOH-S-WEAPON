| Intervals | Frequency (f) | Centers of intervals (Xm) | f.Xm |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 60 - | 1 | 65 | 65 |
| 70 - | 5 | 75 | 375 |
| 80 - | k | 85 | 85 k |
| 90 - | 10 | 95 | 950 |
| 100 - | 6 | 105 | 630 |
| Total | 22 + k | | 2020 + 85 k |

∴ X̄ = Σ (f.Xm) / Σ f = (2020 + 85 k) / (22 + k) = 90
∴ 90 (22 + k) = 2020 + 85 k ∴ 1980 + 90 k = 2020 + 85 k
∴ 90 k - 85 k = 2020 - 1980 ∴ 5 k = 40 ∴ k = 8

2 The median of a frequency distribution with intervals

The median can be estimated from the ascending cumulative frequency curve as follows:
• Determine the order of the median (n/2) where n is the sum of the frequencies on the vertical axis.
• At the median order, draw a horizontal line that intersects the ascending cumulative curve at a point, so that the value of the median (Q2) is the projection of this point on the horizontal axis.
