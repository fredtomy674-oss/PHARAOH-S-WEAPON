The expression (12 X^4 + 18 X^3) can be written as a product of two factors
in more than one way as follows:
3 X (4 X^3 + 6 X^2)
2 X^2 (6 X^2 + 9 X)
6 X^3 (2 X + 3)
6 X (2 X^3 + 3 X^2)
3 X^2 (4 X^2 + 6 X)

(12 X^4 + 18 X^3) by taking out the greatest common factor? Discuss.

Factorizing a polynomial by taking out the greatest common factor (GCF)
A polynomial is a mathematical expression in which all the
exponents of its variables are positive integers.
Examples of polynomials:
• polynomial (monomial): - 3 X Y
• polynomial (binomial): 4 X Y^2 + 2 X
• polynomial (trinomial): 2 X^2 + 3 Y + 8
