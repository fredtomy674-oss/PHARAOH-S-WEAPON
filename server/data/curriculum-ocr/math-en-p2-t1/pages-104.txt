The sum of the measures of the exterior angles (one at each vertex) of the convex hexagon and the convex 12-side polygon are equal.

The previous rule can be deduced by noticing that for any convex polygon with n sides:
• At each vertex there is a pair of angles, one of them is exterior and the other is interior the sum of their measures is 180°.
• The sum of the measures of the pairs of angles at all vertices of the polygon = 180° n
• The sum of the measures of the exterior angles (one at each vertex) is :
180° n - (n - 2) x 180° = 180° n - (180° n - 360°) = 360°

Mathematical formula for the measure of the angle of a regular polygon

In a regular polygon with n sides:
• The measure of the interior angle = (n - 2) x 180° / n
• The measure of the exterior angle at one of its vertices = 360° / n
