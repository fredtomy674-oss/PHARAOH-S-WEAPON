Example 8
If 4x² - 9y² = 115 , 2x - 3y = 5,
find the numerical value of the expression: 2x + 3y

∴ 4x² - 9y² = 115
∴ 5 (2x + 3y) = 115
∴ (2x - 3y) (2x + 3y) = 115
∴ 2x + 3y = 23
