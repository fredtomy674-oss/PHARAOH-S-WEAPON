■ Add to your knowledge
The point of concurrency of the medians of a triangle is called the centroid of the triangle. Also known as the geometric center or the equilibrium point.

Theorem 4-1
The medians of a triangle are concurrent (intersect at one point).
In the given figure:
If AD, BE, and CF are the medians of the triangle ABC,
then AD ∩ BE ∩ CF = {G}
Thus the point G is the concurrency point of
the medians of the triangle ABC.

Theorem 4-2
The centroid of a triangle divides each median in the ratio 2 : 1 from the vertex.
In the given figure:
If G is the intersection point of the medians of
the triangle ABC, then:
AG = 2 GD , AG = 2/3 AD , GD = 1/3 AD

Corollary:
The point which divides a median of a triangle in the ratio 2 : 1 from the vertex is the centroid of the triangle.
In the given figure:
If AD is a median of the triangle ABC
and G ∈ AD such that AG = 2 GD
then G is the centroid of the triangle ABC.

Example 1
