Fourth Case: Angle - Angle - Side (AAS)
Two triangles are congruent if two angles and a non-included side of one triangle are congruent to their corresponding elements of the other triangle.

If ∠ B ≅ ∠ E , ∠ C ≅ ∠ F , AB ≅ DE
then Δ ABC ≅ Δ DEF

■ Note that
In a right-angled triangle, the side opposite to the angle of measure 90° is called the hypotenuse and it is the longest side of the triangle.

Fifth Case: Right angle - Hypotenuse - Side (RHS)
Two right-angled triangles are congruent if the hypotenuse and a side of one triangle are congruent to their corresponding elements of the other triangle.

If m (∠ B) = m (∠ E) = 90°,
AC ≅ DF , AB ≅ DE
then Δ ABC ≅ Δ DEF
