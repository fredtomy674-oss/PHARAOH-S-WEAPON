Corollaries of the axis of symmetry of an isosceles triangle
Corollary:
The median of an isosceles triangle drawn from the vertex bisects the vertex angle and is perpendicular to the base.
If AB = CB,
BD is a median,
then:
① BD ⊥ AC
② m (∠ ABD) = m (∠ CBD)

Corollary:
The bisector of the vertex angle of an isosceles triangle bisects the base and is perpendicular to it.
If AB = CB,
BD bisects ∠ ABC,
then:
① AD = CD
② BD ⊥ AC

Corollary:
The straight line drawn from the vertex of an isosceles triangle perpendicular to the base bisects both the base and the vertex angle.
If AB = CB, BD ⊥ AC,
then:
① AD = DC
② m (∠ ABD) = m (∠ CBD)
