Example 3
In the given figure:
E is the midpoint of each of AB and CD
Prove that: (1) Δ AEC ≅ Δ BED (2) AC // DB

∵ E is the midpoint of each of AB and CD
∴ AE ≅ BE, CE ≅ DE
∵ m (∠ AEC) = m (∠ BED) (vertically opposite angles)
∴ Δ AEC ≅ Δ BED (required 1)
From the congruence, m (∠ A) = m (∠ B)
and they are two alternating angles
∴ AC // DB (required 2)

Third Case: Angle - Side - Angle (ASA)
Two triangles are congruent if two angles and the included side of
one triangle are congruent to their corresponding elements of the
other triangle.

If ∠ A ≅ ∠ D, AC ≅ DF, ∠ C ≅ ∠ F
then Δ ABC ≅ Δ DEF

■ Self-Evaluation (4)
In the following figure:
AB // DC, AC // DE,
BC ≅ EC
Prove that:
Δ ABC ≅ Δ DCE

Example 4
In the given figure:
∠ B ≅ ∠ C, ∠ ADE ≅ ∠ AED, BD ≅ CE
Prove that: Δ ABD ≅ Δ ACE

∵ m (∠ ADB) + m (∠ ADE) = 180°, m (∠ AEC) + m (∠ AED) = 180°
∵ m (∠ ADE) = m (∠ AED) ∴ m (∠ ADB) = m (∠ AEC)
∴ ∠ ADB ≅ ∠ AEC
In Δ ABD and Δ ACE:
∠ B ≅ ∠ C (given), BD ≅ CE (given), ∠ ADB ≅ ∠ AEC (proved)
∴ Δ ABD ≅ Δ ACE
