• Difference between two squares.
• Difference between two cubes.

The pyramids of Giza in Egypt are one of the greatest wonders of the ancient world. The base of the Great Pyramid is a square, and assuming that its area is (X² + 6X + 9) m², can you find its perimeter in terms of X?
