Operations on real numbers
Before studying how to perform mathematical operations (addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division) on real numbers, we will first introduce the concept of like radical terms.
Like radical terms are terms that contain roots with the same index and the same radicand (the number below the root sign)

Examples of like radical terms:
• 4√3 , 2√3
• 3√5 , -3 3√5

Examples of unlike radical terms:
• 3√2 , 2√3
• 2 3√5 , 2√5
