Congruence of two polygons
Two polygons are congruent if the following two conditions are satisfied
together:
1 Their corresponding sides are equal in length.
2 Their corresponding angles are equal in measure.
If ABCD and EFGH are two polygons in which:
1 AB = EF, BC = FG,
CD = GH, AD = EH
2 m (∠ A) = m (∠ E), m (∠ B) = m (∠ F),
m (∠ C) = m (∠ G), m (∠ D) = m (∠ H)
then The polygon ABCD ≅ the polygon EFGH

Congruence of two triangles
The triangle is a polygon with 6 elements (3 sides and 3 angles).
To prove that two triangles are congruent, it is not necessary to prove
that all corresponding elements are congruent. It is sufficient to prove
that three elements, including at least one side, are congruent.
