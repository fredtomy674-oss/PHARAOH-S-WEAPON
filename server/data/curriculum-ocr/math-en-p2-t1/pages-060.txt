Note
The trinomial in the form a x² + b x + c, where (a ≠ 1) can be
factorized by using grouping as follows:
1 Find two factors whose product is (ac) and whose sum is (b).
2 Rewrite (b x) as the sum of these two factors.
3 Use factorization by grouping.

3 x² + 8 x + 4 = 3 x² + 6 x + 2 x + 4 = (3 x² + 6 x) + (2 x + 4)
= 3 x (x + 2) + 2 (x + 2)

Example 3
Factorize each of the following completely:

✓
1 x² + 16 y² - 8 x y - 49 = (x² - 8 x y + 16 y²) - 49
= (x - 4 y)² - (7)²
= (x - 4 y - 7) (x - 4 y + 7)

2 4 m² - n² + 10 n k - 25 k² = 4 m² - (n² - 10 n k + 25 k²)
= 4 m² - (n - 5 k)²
= [2 m - (n - 5 k)] [2 m + (n - 5 k)]
= (2 m - n + 5 k) (2 m + n - 5 k)
