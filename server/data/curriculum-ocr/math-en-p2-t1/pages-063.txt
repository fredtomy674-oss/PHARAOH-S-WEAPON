(1) X^2 - aX + bX - ab = .............
(a) (X - a) (X - b) (b) (X - a) (X + b) (c) (X + a) (X - b) (d) (X + a) (X + b)
(2) If X Y + 5X + 7 Y + 35 = (X + a) (Y + b), what is the value of a - b?
(a) 2 (b) - 2 (c) 12 (d) - 12
(3) If X^3 + 5X^2 - 4X - 20 = (X + a) (X + b) (X + c), what is the value of a + b + c?
(a) 2 (b) 5 (c) 7 (d) 9
(4) If X^2 + a = 12, X + b = 5, what is the numerical value of the polynomial
(X^3 + bX^2 + aX + ab)?
(a) 7 (b) 17 (c) 30 (d) 60
(5) When completing the square for: (X^2 + 9) add .............
(a) ± 3 X (b) ± 6 X (c) ± 9 X (d) ± 12 X
(6) When completing the square for: (X^2 + 12 X) add .............
(a) ± 9 (b) ± 36 (c) 9 (d) 36
(7) If (X^2 + 2X + 2) is one of the two factors of (X^4 + 4), what is the other factor?
(a) X^2 - 2X + 2 (b) X^2 - 2X - 2 (c) X^2 + X + 2 (d) X^2 - X + 2
