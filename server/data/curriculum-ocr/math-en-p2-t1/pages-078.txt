Example 5
Which of the following represents the lengths of the sides of
a right-angled triangle?
1 8 cm, 13 cm, 15 cm
✔ 1 The length of
the longest side = 15
∴ (13)² + (8)² = 233 ,
∴ (15)² = 225
∴ (13)² + (8)² ≠ (15)²
∴ The triangle is not right-angled.
2 24 cm, 7 cm, 25 cm
2 The length of the longest
side = 25
∴ (7)² + (24)² = 625
∴ (25)² = 625
∴ (7)² + (24)² = (25)²
∴ The triangle is right-angled.

Example 6
In the given figure:
XYZM is a concave quadrilateral,
m (∠ XYZ) = 90°.
Find the area of the quadrilateral XYZM.
✔ Construction: Draw XZ
∴ Δ XYZ is right-angled at Y
∴ (XZ)² = (3)² + (4)² = 9 + 16 = 25 ∴ XZ = 5
In Δ XZM :
∴ (XZ)² + (ZM)² = (5)² + (12)² = 25 + 144 = 169 ,
(XM)² = (13)² = 169
∴ (XM)² = (XZ)² + (ZM)² ∴ m (∠ XZM) = 90°
∴ The area of the quadrilateral XYZM = Area of Δ XZM – Area of Δ XYZ
∴ A = (1/2 × 5 × 12) – (1/2 × 3 × 4) = 30 – 6 = 24 cm²
