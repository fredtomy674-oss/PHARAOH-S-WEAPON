The mathematical formula for the sum of the measures of the interior angles of a convex polygon
The sum of the measures of the interior angles of a convex polygon = (n - 2) × 180°, where n is the number of sides of the polygon.
For example:
• The sum of the measures of the interior angles of a convex pentagon = (5 - 2) × 180° = 540°
• The sum of the measures of the interior angles of a convex octagon = (8 - 2) × 180° = 1080°
