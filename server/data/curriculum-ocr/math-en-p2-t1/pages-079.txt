Example 7
Identify the type of each triangle with respect to its angles if the
lengths of its sides are as follows:
1 9 cm, 10 cm, 11 cm
3 1.5 cm, 2 cm, 2.5 cm
2 3 cm, 2√3 cm, 3√3 cm

(1) ∵ The length of the longest side of the triangle = 11 cm
∵ (11)² = 121 , (9)² + (10)² = 81 + 100 = 181
∴ (11)² < (9)² + (10)²
∴ The triangle is acute-angled.

(2) ∵ The length of the longest side of the triangle = 3√3 cm
∵ (3√3)² = 27 , (3)² + (2√3)² = 9 + 12 = 21
∴ (3√3)² > (3)² + (2√3)²
∴ The triangle is obtuse-angled.

(3) ∵ The length of the longest side of the triangle = 2.5 cm
∵ (2.5)² = 6.25 , (1.5)² + (2)² = 2.25 + 4 = 6.25
∴ (2.5)² = (1.5)² + (2)²
∴ The triangle is right-angled.
