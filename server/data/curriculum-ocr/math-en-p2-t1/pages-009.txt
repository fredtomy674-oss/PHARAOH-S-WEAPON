Example (3)
Nature: The golden rectangle is noticed in the
shell of a marine organism, and the ratio of its
length to its width is equal to 1+√5 / 2
Estimate this value.

First, estimate the value of √5 as follows:
∴ The number 5 lies between the two perfect square numbers 4 and 9
∴ 4 < 5 < 9 ∴ √4 < √5 < √9 ∴ 2 < √5 < 3
∴ The number 5 is closer to the number 4 than to the number 9
∴ √5 is closer to the number 2.
Use « √5 ≈ 2 » to estimate the value of 1+√5 / 2
∴ 1+√5 / 2 ≈ 1+2 / 2 ∴ 1+√5 / 2 ≈ 1.5

Real numbers
The set of real numbers is the set that consists of the union of the
set of rational numbers and the set of irrational numbers, and is
denoted by the symbol R
i.e. R = Q ∪ Q' where Q ∩ Q' = ∅
