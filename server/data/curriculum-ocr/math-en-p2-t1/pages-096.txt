Equilateral triangle

An equilateral triangle is a triangle whose three sides are congruent.

Corollary:
If the triangle is equilateral, then it is equiangular (has three congruent angles).
In Δ ABC:
If
AB = AC = BC,
then:
∠ A ≅ ∠ B ≅ ∠ C

Corollary:
If a triangle is equiangular, then it is equilateral.
In Δ ABC:
If
∠ A ≅ ∠ B ≅ ∠ C,
then:
AB = AC = BC

Example (3)
In the given figure:
ABC is an equilateral triangle,
AC ∩ ED = {F}, CF = CD
Find the value of x

∴ Δ ABC is an equilateral triangle.
∴ m (∠ ACB) = m (∠ A) = m (∠ B) = 60°
∴ m (∠ FCD) = 180° - 60° = 120°
∴ Δ FCD is an isosceles triangle in which CF = CD
∴ m (∠ D) = m (∠ CFD) = 180° - 120° / 2 = 30°
∴ ∠ AED is an exterior angle of Δ EBD
∴ m (∠ AED) = m (∠ B) + m (∠ D) = 60° + 30° = 90°
∴ x = 90

Axis of symmetry of a line segment
The axis of symmetry of a line segment is the perpendicular straight line passing through its midpoint.
If the straight line l ⊥ AB, the straight line l bisects AB,
then the straight line l is the axis of symmetry of AB
