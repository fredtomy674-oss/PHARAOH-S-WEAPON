• Factorizing the trinomial that represents a perfect square is to write it as
the product of two equal factors
(i.e., the square of one of its equal factors).
That is:
a^2 + 2 a b + b^2 = (a + b)^2
a^2 - 2 a b + b^2 = (a - b)^2
