Irrational numbers
An irrational number is a number that cannot be written in the form of a/b where a and b are integers, b ≠ 0, and the set of irrational numbers is denoted by the symbol Q'.

Examples of irrational numbers:
• The square root of any number that is not a perfect square, such as: √2 where √2 ≈ 1.414213562 ...
• The cube root of any number that is not a perfect cube, such as: ∛5 where ∛5 ≈ 1.709975947 ...

■ Note That
Calculators give approximate values for irrational numbers such as:
√2, ∛5 where the decimal fraction is infinite and non-recurring, so such numbers cannot be written as rational numbers.
