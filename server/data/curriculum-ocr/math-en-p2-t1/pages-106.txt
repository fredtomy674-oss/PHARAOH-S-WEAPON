Amal's solution:
(n - 2) 180° = (5 - 2) 180°
= 3 × 180° = 540°
The measure of the exterior angle E
E = 540° / 5 = 108°

Samar's solution:
There are 10 exterior angles of the pentagon.
The measure of the exterior angle E
E = 360° / 10 = 36°
