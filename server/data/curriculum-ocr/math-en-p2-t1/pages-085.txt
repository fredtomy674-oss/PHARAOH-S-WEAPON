Theorem 3-3 (Theorem of the midsegment of a triangle)
The midsegment of a triangle is parallel to the third side and its length is equal to half the length of that side.

If DE is a midsegment of Δ ABC,
then : 1 DE // BC
2 DE = 1/2 BC

Example 2
In the given figure:
Find with proof the length of EF.

∴ Δ ABD is right-angled at D
∴ (AD)² = (AB)² - (BD)² = (13)² - (5)² = 144
∴ AD = √144 = 12
∴ m (∠ ADB) = m (∠ EFB) = 90° (and they are corresponding angles)
∴ AD // EF
In Δ ADC : ∵ E is the midpoint of AC, AD // EF
∴ F is the midpoint of DC
∴ EF = 1/2 AD
∴ EF = 1/2 x 12 = 6 cm.

Example 3
In the given figure:
If D is the midpoint of AB, E is the midpoint of AC,
G is the midpoint of DF,
prove that: BC = 4 GH

In Δ ABC :
∵ D is the midpoint of AB, E is the midpoint of AC
∴ DE // BC, DE = 1/2 BC
In Δ FDE :
∵ G is the midpoint of DF, DE // GH ∴ H is the midpoint of EF
∴ GH = 1/2 DE ∴ GH = 1/2 (1/2 BC) = 1/4 BC ∴ BC = 4 GH
