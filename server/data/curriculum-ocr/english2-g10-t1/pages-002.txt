Introduction

English as a second foreign language offers students another
way to connect with people, explore ideas, and prepare for future
opportunities. This book is designed to help secondary stage students
develop the language and confidence to communicate in meaningful
situations.

Each unit brings together listening, speaking, reading, and writing
through topics related to students' lives and the world around them.
Vocabulary and grammar are presented in context and practised
through discussions, texts, writing tasks, and practical activities.
As you progress, you will learn to express opinions, give reasons,
compare ideas, and communicate with greater independence.

The book also encourages you to think critically, work with others,
and use information and technology responsibly. You will have
opportunities to connect what you learn in class with situations
beyond it and to reflect on your progress.

Language learning takes practice, curiosity, and a willingness to try.
We hope this book supports you in becoming a more confident English
user and helps you see how another language can enrich your learning
and broaden your choices.
