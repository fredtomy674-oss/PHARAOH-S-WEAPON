My School Life

Learning Outcomes

Reading
- Identify main ideas in simple texts about school life
- Understand descriptions of daily school activities
- Infer meanings of new vocabulary from context

Listening
- Listen for key information in short conversations about school
- Understand simple instructions and classroom language

Speaking
- Ask and answer questions about school subjects and activities
- Talk about favorite subjects and school routines
- Express opinions about school projects and habits

Language
- Use present simple questions correctly (Do / Does)
- Apply basic grammar in speaking and writing
- Use common vocabulary related to school (lab, library, ICT, etc.)

Writing
- Write short paragraphs about school day and school projects
- Write simple emails about daily school life
- Use correct punctuation and capital letters
