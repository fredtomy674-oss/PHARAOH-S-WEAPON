Tip: Writing Email
• Start with a greeting: Hi / Hello
• Ask about the person: How are you?
• Write short sentences
• Talk about one topic (your school day)
• Use simple words (go, eat, study, play)
• Use and / then to connect ideas
• End with a closing: Best wishes / See you
• Write your name at the end
