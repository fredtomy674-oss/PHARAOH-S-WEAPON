Tip: Present Simple
Questions

We use present simple ques-
tions to ask about daily life.
Yes / No Questions
Use: Do / Does + subject +
base verb
• Do → I / you / we / they
• Does → he / she / it
Examples:
1. Do you like computers?
Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
2. Does Lina use a computer?
Yes, she does. / No, she
doesn't.
