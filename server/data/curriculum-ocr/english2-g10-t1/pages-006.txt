UNIT
1
Back to School

Learning Outcomes
Reading
• Identify key information in
self-introductions and
cultural profiles

• Understand descriptions of
first-day-of-school experiences
and social interactions

• Infer the meaning of target
vocabulary related to
personal identity

Listening
• Listen for key information in
a text where student
introduces themselves

• Distinguish between various
personal details and cultural
identifiers in spoken exchanges

Speaking
• Participate in simple exchanges
to introduce oneself and others

• Engage in meaningful
discussions about personal
identity and native languages

• Ask and answer personal
questions accurately

Language
• Use the verb "to be" (am, is, are)
in affirmative and interrogative
forms

• Apply target vocabulary
(name, country, language,
culture, proud) correctly

Writing
• Write a Social Media Post
(4-5 sentences)
