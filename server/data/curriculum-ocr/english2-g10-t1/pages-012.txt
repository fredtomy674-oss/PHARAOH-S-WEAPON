Tip: Sounds in Words
Short/Long Vowels:
A vowel can make a short
sound or say its letter
name (long sound).
Short a → bag, stand,
hand
Long a → name, raise,
age
Initial Consonants: The
single starting sound of a
word.
b → book, bag, boy
p → pen, pencil, page
Simple Blends: Two
consonants together at the
beginning.
cl → class, close, clock
pl → please, play, plus
