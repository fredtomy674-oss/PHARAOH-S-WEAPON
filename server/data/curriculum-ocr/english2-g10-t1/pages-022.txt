Tip: Present Simple Tense
We use present simple tense to talk
about daily routine.
Affirmative form
Subject + base verb + (s/es for he/she/
it)
• I play football every weekend.
• She works in a bank
Negative form:
Subject + do/does + not + base verb
• I don't play football on Sundays.
• She doesn't work in a bank.
