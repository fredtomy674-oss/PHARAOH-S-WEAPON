Reading

Tip: What does an “About Me” Post
mean online?
An "About me" post is a short text you
write on social media. People write it to
introduce themselves to new friends on-
line. They often share their name, where
they are from, the languages they speak, and
what they love about their culture.

2 Read the following post
then answer the questions

My Social Media Profile

Hello everyone!
My name is Sara. I am a student.
I am from Egypt.
I speak Arabic. It is a beautiful language.
I am so proud of my Egyptian culture and history.
I am excited to meet new friends here on social media.
Tell me about your country!

A. Read and write T (True) or F (False)
1. Sara is a teacher.
2. Sara is from Egypt.
3. She speaks English only.
4. Arabic is a beautiful language.
5. Sara is proud of her culture.
