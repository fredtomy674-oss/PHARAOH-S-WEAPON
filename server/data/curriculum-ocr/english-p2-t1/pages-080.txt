When they reached the Hidden Island, they found strange plants, colorful birds, and
mysterious caves. Hany suggested, "Let's split into pairs and explore, but stay close
and safe."

They discovered beautiful shells, shiny
stones, and even a waterfall. Faris led his
group carefully, making sure no one got
hurt. Malik used his creativity to draw
maps of the island, while Sami shared his
discovery of a hidden cave.

When they felt tired, they sat by the
water and shared stories. "I appreciate
this day," said Sami. "I feel free to be
myself."

The friends smiled, knowing that
freedom wasn't about doing anything
they wanted, but about respecting one
another, listening, and working together.
