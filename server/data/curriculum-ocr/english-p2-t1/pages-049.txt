The Little Inventor
Before you read, discuss in pairs
1 Answer the following question.
- Do you think Amal will win the competition?
2 Work in pairs to guess what the words in the box mean.
- creativity
- determination
- display
- inspire
- honest

3 Read the following text. Why did Amal feel proud at the end of the fair?

On the day of the fair, all the children
displayed their work. Amal stood
proudly with her robot. When she
presented it, the robot spoke: "Be
yourself. Never give up!"

Everyone clapped. The children who
once laughed at her now admired Amal's
hard work and creativity. The teacher
smiled and said, "Amal, you showed,
determination, and honesty. You
worked hard without giving up, and you
stayed true to who you are."

Amal felt proud, not because she won
a prize, but because she had expressed
herself honestly, used digital tools
responsibly, and showed empathy to
others.

From that day on, Amal inspired
others to follow their dreams, express
themselves, and help one another.
