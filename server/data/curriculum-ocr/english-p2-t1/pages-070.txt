LESSON 6 My Performance Review

Before You Write, Let's Get Ready
Target Language
1. The exam was so easy, so all students succeeded.
2. I didn't go out because I was studying my lessons.
3. Although Omar is rich, he's wearing old clothes.
4. It's too hot, but I'll spend the day out.

Target Vocabulary
Try to include at least 6 of these words in your email:
artist - symbol - amazing - creativity - imagination - star - actress - original

Helpful Hints
- Start your email with:
Hi [friend's name].
- Say why you are writing.
- Use short and simple sentences.
- Finish your email with a closing sentence/phrase like: Write back soon! or Talk to you later!

Writing
Read and complete the following email with words from the box above.

New Message
To : Eyad
From : Ayman
Subject : Does Art Only Mean Painting?

Hi Eyad,
How are you?
I'm pleased to write this email to you. I've read an amazing article about art.
Art is much more than just painting. It is a way to express (1) ............... and (2) ............... .
An (3) ............... can be a painter, a musician, a writer, or even an actor. In Egypt, one famous movie star is Omar Sharif. He was known all over the world and acted in many (4) ............... movies. His talent and passion for acting made him a (5) ............... of Egyptian cinema. Another great star is Amina Rizk, a famous Egyptian (6) ............... known for her strong roles in theater and movies. Her (7) ............... roles as a kind mother touched the hearts of many people all over the Arab world.
What do you think?
Write back soon,
Yours,
Ayman
