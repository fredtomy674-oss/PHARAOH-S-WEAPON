LESSON 2 Staying Safe Online

Before you start
Think and discuss in pairs
1 Answer the following questions.
1. What should you do before posting something online?
2. What should your strong password include?

Listening
2 Listen to a short podcast about "Online Safety".
- What should you do if you experience something uncomfortable online?

3 Listen to the podcast again. In pairs, match the words with their meanings.
A
1. private
2. trusted
3. experience
4. adult

B
something that is personal and not meant to be shared with everyone
the knowledge or skill that you get from doing, seeing or feeling things
a person who is fully grown up
believed to be honest

4 Listen again. Then, in pairs, put (T) True or (F) False.
1. You should think carelessly before posting personal information online.
2. It's important to keep our passwords private.
3. If something online makes you uncomfortable, ignore it.
4. A strong password is like a secret code.

What should you do before posting something online?
I should make sure it is respectful and won't hurt anyone.

Speaking
5 With your partner, ask and answer these questions.
1. What are some ways to create a strong password?
2. What do privacy settings allow you to do?
