Language in Use

6 Study the following examples.
- People create a digital trail. (Active)
- A digital trail is created by people. (Passive)
- You should limit the use of social media. (Active)
- The use of social media should be limited. (Passive)

7 Change the sentences from active to passive.
1. People upload videos on social media.
2. You should protect your privacy on the internet.
3. Teachers explain the rules of behavior in the classroom.
4. We use strong passwords to protect our data.
5. Students should follow the school rules.

Tip
Present Simple Passive
object + am, is, are + p.p. ..
• People share photos online. (Active)
• Photos are shared online. (Passive)
Modal Passive
object + Modal + be + p.p. ..
• You should keep your passwords secret. (Active)
• Your passwords should be kept secret. (Passive)
