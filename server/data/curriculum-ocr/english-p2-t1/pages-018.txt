Tip
We use the present continuous to talk about:
• Actions happening right now
• Temporary actions
• Future arrangements :
They are actions that are already arranged and confirmed.
Rule: Subject + am/is/are + verb + ing + future time expression
Ex.: I am traveling to Luxor tomorrow at 9 p.m.
Time adverbs: now, at the moment, today, this week, right now, next (month)
