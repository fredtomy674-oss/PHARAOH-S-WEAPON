Punctuation Marks
• Learn
An apostrophe is a punctuation mark we use to:
1. Show possession (something belongs to someone)
Examples:
• This is Noha’s book. (The book belongs to Noha)
If the noun is plural and ends in “s”, just add the apostrophe after the "s".
• The teachers’ room. (Room of many teachers)

2. Make contractions (short forms by joining words)
Examples:
• (I am = I’m) (He is – He’s) (do not = don’t)

• Practice

1. She ll come soon.
2. The girls bags are over there.
3. This is Amir s mobile phone.
