LESSON 5 My Dream Job!

Before You Write: Let's Get Ready
Target language
• I am going to study engineering.
• I'm going to learn how to design buildings.

Target vocabulary
Try to include at least 6 of these words in your review:
dream – plan – career – succeed – interview – path –
ambition – skill – talent – opportunity – success

Writing
• Read and complete from the box.
Helping - caring - difference - study - treats

Tip
To write a review:
- describe what you are writing
about.
- write the good and bad sides.
- write what you think of it.

Your task
- Write a review of (80-100) words about your dream job, and what you are going to do
to achieve it.
You can use these guiding questions to help you:
1. What is your dream job?
2. Why do you want this job?
