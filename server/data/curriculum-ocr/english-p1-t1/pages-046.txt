Tip
The Past Simple
• We use the past simple to talk about actions that started and ended in the past.
• We add "-ed" to regular verbs in the past simple.
• Irregular verbs aren't formed by adding "-ed" to the infinitive form.
• Dr Samira Mousa was a hero.
• We moved to Luxor three years ago.
• Nada didn't go to school yesterday.
• Where did you go last summer?

Tip
Verb to be in the past simple
I / he / she / it → was
we / you / they → were
