Tips
Suffixes
A suffix is a letter or a group of letters that you add to the end of a word to make a new word.
• think (verb) +er → "thinker"

Prefixes
A prefix is a group of letters we add to the beginning of a word to change its meaning.
(un) + happy → unhappy.
(ir) + responsible → irresponsible
• The root of the word is the base word before adding a prefix or suffix.
