Activity 1 Circle Sharing:
Sit in groups, and let’s look back at everything we
learned in this unit!

Activity 2 Group Discussion:
Discuss these questions in your group:
1. What is your dream job? Why do you want it?
2. What skills or talents do you need for this job?
3. What are you going to do to reach this goal?
4. Who inspires you to follow this dream?

Activity 3 My Dream Job Mind Map:
Make a creative mind map to show your future dreams!
• Instructions:
- Write your dream job in the center of the page.
- Draw branches for these ideas: Plans, Predictions/Wants, Skills and Talents, and Ambitions.
- Use your vocabulary words.
- Write at least three sentences using “going to”.
- Add drawings and colors to make it fun and creative!
• Presentation:
- Share your mind map with your group first, then share it with the whole class.
