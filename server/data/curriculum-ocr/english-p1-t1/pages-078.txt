Tip
The future with (be) + going to
• We use (be) + going to + infinitive form to express :
Future plans
- We are going to buy a car next month.
- He is going to have a party next week.
Future intentions
- I am going to watch a movie tonight.
- We are going to go to the club in the evening.
Prediction with evidence
- Look at the clouds! It's going to rain.
