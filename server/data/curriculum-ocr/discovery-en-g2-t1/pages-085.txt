A BUMPY CODE
A BRAILLE MESSAGE
A special code was developed for soldiers to read messages
in the dark. Louis Braille improved this code. He was blinded
as a child and invented his system as a teenager. He used a
system of raised dots, or bumps, in a three by two cell.
1 4
2 5
3 6
People read this code, called braille, by feeling the pattern of the bumps.
Different combinations of the six bumps are created to form letters, numbers,
punctuation, and even musical notes.
