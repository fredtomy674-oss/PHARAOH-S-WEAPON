I am very busy every day! First, my sister and I get ready for school. We have to
share a bathroom. My sister Sara takes a long time to get ready. She makes us
late sometimes.
My mom tells us to set a timer.
This way, Sara will get ready
faster.
Before we leave, we eat a quick breakfast.
Grandma made us ful. I am still hungry.
Grandma makes me a fruit salad as well. My
sister and I walk to school together. The road
can be very busy. I ask her to hold my hand.
This way I stay safe. I am happy when we
finally get to school. I wonder what we will
do today!
