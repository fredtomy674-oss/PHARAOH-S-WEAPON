NOUR’S NEW CLASSMATE
Nour arrived home from school.
“Good afternoon, Grandma. We have a
new student in our class. Her name is
Rana. She is blind. That means she cannot
see,” Nour tells Grandma.
“Wow. How interesting,” Grandma replies.
“Even though she cannot see, she can still
read,” Nour exclaims.
“How can she do that?” Grandma asks.
“She reads special books. Her books are written in braille. Braille letters are
dots that she can feel on the page. Those dots tell her the letters to make
words. It is amazing!” Nour says.
“That is indeed amazing. So instead of looking at the words and reading them,
she feels the words. But our computers and cell phones do not have braille
letters on them. I wonder how she communicates with others,” Grandma says.
