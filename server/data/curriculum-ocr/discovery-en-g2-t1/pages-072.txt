NOUR CELEBRATES EID AL FITR
Nour quickly put on the new clothes she just received. She was ready to go out
and celebrate Eid Al Fitr with her friends.
Today was the third day of Eid Al Fitr and she could not wait to get to the
carnival. Hossam was going to meet her there with his family.
