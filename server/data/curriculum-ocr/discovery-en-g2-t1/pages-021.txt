BABY BIRDS
At my home I can see another baby. These
babies are living in a nest. They are birds.
Yesterday there were three eggs in the nest.
Now I can see three new baby birds. I can see
the mother bird taking care of the babies.
She brings the babies food when they cry.
She sits on them to keep them warm.I cannot
wait to watch the baby birds grow up.
