Interdisciplinary Project

Design Requirements
Your design turns a plastic bottle or plastic bag into something new.
Your team's final sketch lists the materials needed and how the design will work.
Your group collaborates to use your materials listed to create a repurposed plastic product.

Sketching Our Design
Sketch your initial idea for how your team can repurpose your plastic bag or plastic water bottle into something new that others could use. After you and your teammates share your ideas, vote on one final design to create, and label the materials needed. Add a sentence to the bottom of your sketch describing how your prototype will work.

With your team, discuss these two questions about your ideas:
• What do you like about these ideas?
• Where can you make improvements to the design?

Circle the final design that you will create.
