CONCEPT
1.1 Plant Needs

Student Objectives

By the end of this concept:

I can use evidence to argue that plants use specialized structures to obtain
the materials that they need to grow from sun, air, and water.

I can develop a model of how energy moves through plants.

I can develop a model of plant processes that use natural resources to
complete life processes.

I can compare the structure and function of the vascular system in plants
with the circulatory system in humans.
