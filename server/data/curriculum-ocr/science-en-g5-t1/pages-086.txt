Think About the Activity
After you discuss your models with the class, think of other ways you could model the
different states of matter. Could you use movement to show how the particles of solids,
liquids, and gases behave? Once you have analyzed your model and considered other
modeling methods, write your answers to the questions that follow.

Describe the arrangement of particles in the different states of matter you modeled in
this investigation.

What is matter composed of?

materials in each state will behave?
