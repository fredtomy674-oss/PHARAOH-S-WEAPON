Measuring Matter

You have learned a lot about using measurements to compare materials and properties
of matter. Now it is your turn to share what you know. The following table shows a group
of measurements that are taken.

Note that mass is measured in grams (g), length is measured in centimeters (cm), and
volume is measured in milliliters (mL). Examine the data in the table carefully and look for
patterns in the data.

| | Mass (g) | Length (cm) | Volume (mL) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Material 1 | 189 | 37 | 100 |
| Material 2 | 150 | 55 | 115 |
| Material 3 | 99 | 23 | 5 |

Based on the data in the table, select the correct words to make each statement true.

1. ____________________ [Material 1/Material 3] contains more matter than Material 2.

2. ____________________ [Material 2/Material 3] is longer than Material 1.

3. ____________________ [Material 2/Material 3] takes up more space than Material 1.
