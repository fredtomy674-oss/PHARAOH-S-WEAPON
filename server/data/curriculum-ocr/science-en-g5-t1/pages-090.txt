Student Objectives
By the end of this concept:

I can classify materials based on their properties and describe patterns in
the properties of similar materials.

I can choose the appropriate tools to measure the size and volume of
different kinds of materials in different states of matter.

I can plan and conduct investigations to gather and record information
about the properties of various materials.

I can analyze data to identify unknown materials.
