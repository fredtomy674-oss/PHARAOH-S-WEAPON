Seed Dispersal
An interesting thing Dr. Barak has learned about plants is that different plants
need different ways to transport or disperse their seeds. One plant has seeds
that are really sticky. Their seeds can stick to your clothing. You never know
where you might leave them. Other plants have seeds that are dispersed by
the wind. These seeds are released from the plant when the plant is ready. The
seeds fly away to new habitats to grow in other places.
