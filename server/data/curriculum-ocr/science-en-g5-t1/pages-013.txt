Unit Project: Build a Miniature Ecosystem
In this project, you will use what you know about how living things
interact with their environment to build a miniature ecosystem.

Producers in a Terrarium

Ask Questions About the Problem
Think about the different types of organisms that are found in a healthy ecosystem.
Consider how they depend on the other living things in the community. What are
some of the non-living things that are critical for survival in an ecosystem? Write
some questions you can ask to learn more about ecosystems. As you learn about
the components of a food web and the interactions that organisms have with their
environment, record the answers to your questions.
