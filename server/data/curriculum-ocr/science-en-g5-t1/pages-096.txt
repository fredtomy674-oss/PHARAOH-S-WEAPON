Properties of Matter

You have now learned a variety of ways to describe and measure matter. Now, read the text to discover more ways that matter can be observed and measured. Once you have completed the reading, circle the properties of matter you can observe and measure. Add your notes to your placemat.

Physical Properties of Matter

Matter has many properties that you can describe. Color, shape, odor, and texture are examples of physical properties that you can observe with your five senses. You can use words such as rough, blue, floral, round, and sweet to describe these properties.

A Burning Match

Chemical Properties of Matter

The ability to burn and the ability to rust are properties that describe how matter interacts with other matter. These are called chemical properties. An important feature of chemical properties is that they are only measurable by changing the material. For example, a chemical property of paper is that it is flammable.
