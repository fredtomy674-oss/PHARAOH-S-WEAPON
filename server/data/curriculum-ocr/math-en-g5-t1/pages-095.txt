BUILD
Keep the Leftovers

Play the game with your partner to practice checking division answers using
multiplication.
1. Decide who goes first.
2. Player 1 starts with 200 and chooses a divisor from the list. Players cross
out the divisor so it cannot be used again.
3. Player 1 solves the division problem and announces the solution.
Example: 200 ÷ 17 = 11 R13.
4. Player 2 uses multiplication and addition to check. Example:
17 × 11 = 187. 187 + 13 = 200.
5. When you and your partner agree on the solution, write the equation on
the record sheet. Circle the remainder and write Player 1's initials next to
the problem.
