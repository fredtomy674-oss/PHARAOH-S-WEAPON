LESSON 3
Multiplying Tenths by Tenths

Learning Targets
• I can use models to represent multiplying decimals.
• I can explain patterns when multiplying tenths by tenths.

ACCESS
The Debate Read the story. Choose the student you think is correct. Use words and numbers to explain your thinking.

Asked teacher asked them what place value the product for decimal numbers in they multiplied two numbers in the Tenths place, such as 0.5 and 0.7.

Kamal said the Tenths place because multiplication makes numbers greater. So Tenths times Tenths would be adding a lot of Tenths together.

Nadia said the Hundredths place, because the product of two numbers in the Tenths place would have a product in the Hundredths place.

Who do you think is correct? Explain your thinking.
