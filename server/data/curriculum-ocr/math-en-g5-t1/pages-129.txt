BUILD
Order Matters Use the order of operations to evaluate each expression.

Basic Order of Operations
1. Multiply or divide from left to right.
2. Add or subtract from left to right.

1. 82.43 x 3.1 + 4.05 ÷ 0.01 - 2.5
2. 56.5 x 2.3 - 15 + 12.7
3. 597.8 ÷ 6.1 + 13 x 1.7
4. 90.7 + 116.6 x 0.1 x 2 - 20
5. 1,403.5 - 12.3 ÷ 0.01 + 9.8

One Step at a Time Evaluate each expression, one step at a time, with your class
or group. Be sure to rewrite the expression after each step.

1. 145.42 - 7.11 x 10 + 13.2
2. 35 x 0.1 + 89.14 ÷ 0.1
3. 102.15 + 6 ÷ 1.2 - 34 x 2.3
