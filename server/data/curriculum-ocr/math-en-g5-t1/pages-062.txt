ACCESS
Diving in the Red Sea Work with your teacher to read the passage and respond to the questions.
Scuba diving is a popular tourist attraction in the Red Sea. One of the more popular dives is to an 80-meter-long steamship that sank in 1876. The crew worked for 14 hours to dislodge the boat, but it capsized and sank to the seabed 30 meters below.

Free Divers

1. Shadi and Taha went diving to the steamship. They each stopped at intervals of equal depths to check their gear. Shadi dove to the stern at 30 meters below the surface. What are all the options of intervals he could take? (Stopping every 1 m is not practical, nor is going the entire distance.)
A. 2 m, 3 m, 5 m
B. 2 m, 3 m, 5 m, 6 m
C. 2 m, 3 m, 5 m, 6 m, 10 m, 15 m
D. 2 m, 3 m, 5 m, 6 m, 10 m, 12 m

2. Taha dove to the hull at a depth of 15 meters. What are the options of intervals he could take? (Stopping every 1 m is not practical, nor is going the entire distance.)
A. 3 m, 5 m
B. 2 m, 3 m, 5 m
C. 2 m, 3 m, 5 m, 6 m
D. 2 m, 3 m, 5 m, 6 m, 10 m

Challenge If both divers stop at equivalent equal intervals, what is the greatest distance they can both dive before stopping?
A. 2 m
