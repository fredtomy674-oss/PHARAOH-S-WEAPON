Sinai Peninsula
Sinai Peninsula is triangular and has an area of about 60,000 square kilometers. It is connected to Africa on the west by the Gulf of Suez, which is approximately 275 kilometers long. On the east it is connected to Asia by Aqaba Gulf that is approximately 180 km long. There are approximately 600,000 people living on Sinai Peninsula.

Mariam wrote two equations to compare the lengths of the two gulfs.
Here are her equations.
• 180 + x = 275
• x = 275 - 180

What does the letter x represent in these equations?
A. the length in kilometers of one gulf
B. the difference in kilometers between the two lengths
C. the width of Sinai Peninsula
D. the distance in kilometers between the gulfs
