Tahya Misr Bridge

The Tahya Misr Bridge was built in 2016 in Cairo. It serves as a connector across the Nile from northern and eastern Cairo to western Cairo. The bridge is 540 meters long and 67.3 meters wide. It holds the world record for the widest cable-stayed bridge in the world.
