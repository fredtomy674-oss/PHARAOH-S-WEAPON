ACTIVITY
Winner Takes All

Play the game in groups of two to practice estimating quotients.
Object: Collect all the cards.

1. Each player shuffles a deck of 12 cards and places the cards face down.
2. Each player places the top card in the center space at the same time, revealing each division problem.
3. Each player estimates the quotient using compatible numbers and then shares their estimate with the other player. Players should check each other's work.
4. The player with the greater estimated quotient takes both cards and adds them to the bottom of his or her own pile.
5. If there is a tie, both players turn over the next card on their piles and repeat the process. The winner of that round takes all four cards.
6. Play continues until one player has all the cards.

CONNECT
