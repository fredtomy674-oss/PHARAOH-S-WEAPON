Advanced Order of Operations
1. For operations within parentheses
a. multiply or divide from left to right
b. add or subtract from left to right
2. For operations within brackets
a. multiply or divide from left to right
b. add or subtract from left to right
3. For operations outside of parentheses or brackets
a. multiply or divide from left to right
b. add or subtract from left to right
