Blood pressure is the force exerted by the blood as it circulates on a unit
area of the walls of blood vessels. Blood pressure is expressed by two values:
systolic pressure (when the heart contracts) and diastolic pressure (when the
heart relaxes).
Normal blood pressure for healthy adults is about 120 / 80 mmHg.
Maintaining this rate is essential for delivering oxygen and nutrients to
tissues and for the overall health of the heart and blood vessels.
Third: Wind
Atmospheric pressure affects
weather and climate because the
difference in atmospheric pressure
between two regions at the same
horizontal level leads to air movement,
with air moving from the high-pressure
area to the low-pressure area, resulting
in wind blowing.
There are several wind systems
at the Earth’s surface, including polar
winds, which are dry and cold winds that blow from high-pressure areas
around the North and South Poles to low-pressure areas in the subpolar
regions.
