Effect of the Increase in CO 2 ratio in Water on Aquatic Organisms
An increase in the percentage of carbon dioxide (CO ₂ ) in water can have
several negative effects on aquatic organisms, including:
1. Acidification: When carbon dioxide gas level is high in the atmosphere,
it dissolves in greater amounts in the water, leading to an increase in
the formation of carbonic acid (H ₂ CO ₃ ) and a decrease in the water's
pH value. This acidification can be harmful to many species of aquatic
organisms, especially in sensitive life stages such as the egg and larva
stages.
2. Impairing Respiration: High levels of carbon dioxide in water can reduce
the amount of dissolved oxygen necessary for aquatic organisms to
breathe.
3. Weakening Support Structure: Support structure in living organisms is
