Effect of Humidity on Living Organisms:
Some vital processes in living organisms are affected by the humidity
level in the atmospheric air. With increased relative humidity around plants,
the rate of transpiration decreases, reducing the rate of water and salt
uptake from the roots to the leaves. In animals, the rate of sweat evaporation
decreases, reducing the efficiency of lowering body temperature.
