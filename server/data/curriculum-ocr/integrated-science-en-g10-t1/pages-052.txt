Effect of the Decrease in CO 2 ratio in Water on Aquatic Organisms:
1. Reducing Efficiency Rate of Photosynthesis and Energy Production: Aquatic
plants and algae need carbon dioxide to perform photosynthesis.
A decrease in the availability of carbon dioxide in water leads to a decrease
in their sugar formation and weakening their energy production.
2. Impacting Food Chains: A decrease in carbon dioxide levels in water leads
to a lower rate of sugar formation, such as glucose, which limits the
energy reserves in producer organisms like phytoplankton and algae.
Consequently, the amount of energy available to living organisms at
higher levels of the food chain decreases.
3. Disrupting Water pH Balance: Low concentrations of carbon dioxide may
lead to an increase in the pH value of water, which negatively affects
sensitive species that are adapted to a specific pH range.
