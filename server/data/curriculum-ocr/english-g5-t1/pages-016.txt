Pronunciation
1 Read and learn.
A consonant cluster is a sequence of two or more consonant sounds
without vowels, such as str- in strong or bl- in black. They can appear
at the beginning, middle, or end of words.
Examples: tr ee, br anches, cr ocodiles , spr ing
