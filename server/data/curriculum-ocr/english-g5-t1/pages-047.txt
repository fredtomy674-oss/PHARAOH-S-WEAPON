Past Simple
We use the past simple to talk about finished actions or
events in the past.
Example: In Ancient Egypt, the Nile River flood ed every year.
We use
“ didn't
” + the infinitive form of the verb to make
negative sentences in the past simple.
Example: The Ancient Egyptians didn ’ t have modern tools
to help them.
But with verb "to be” , we put “not”
after “was/were” .
Example: Droughts happened when there was not enough
rain.
We use “Did ...?” or “Wh-word + did ...?” +the infinitive form
of the verb to make the question form.
Example: What did the Ancient Egyptians do when the river
flooded?
But with verb "to be” , we use “Was/Were ...?” or “Wh-word +
was/were ...?”.
Example: Were there sandstorms in the past?
