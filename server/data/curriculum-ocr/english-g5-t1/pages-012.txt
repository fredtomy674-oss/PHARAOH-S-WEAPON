Adverbs of Frequency
We use adverbs of frequency to talk about how often something
happens.
sometimes
often
usually
always
never
rarely
(50%) (70%) (90%) (100%) (20%) (0%)
The adverbs come before the main verb like ( eat, play, go )
and after the verb “ to be ” like ( am, is, are ).
Examples:
Snakes never eat plants.
Lizards are sometimes dangerous.
