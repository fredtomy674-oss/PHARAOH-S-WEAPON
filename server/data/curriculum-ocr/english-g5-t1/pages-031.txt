Our Body's Super Pump!
We have an amazing body. The heart is one of the most important organs of
our body. It is about the size of your fist . The heart’s job is to pump blood to all
parts of the body.
Blood carries oxygen and nutrients to every cell. It also takes away waste like
carbon dioxide . The heart pumps blood through blood vessels , which are like
small tubes.
The heart is a strong muscle that keeps us alive. Too much stress can hurt
your heart, so it’s important to keep it healthy. You can take care of your heart
by eating good food, sleeping well, and drinking a lot of water.
Also, exercise helps your heart pump better and keeps you from getting sick.
If you follow these tips, your heart will stay strong. It may be small, but it is very
powerful!
