Billie Wind was a young Native American girl who lived in Florida. She loved
science and dreamed of becoming a scientist when she grew up. But her
family believed that the Earth could "talk" and share wisdom with those who
listened to it. They said the wind, the water, and the animals all had something
to teach. Billie did not believe this. She thought these were just old stories
with no real meaning.
