Lesson 3
Pronunciation
1 Read and learn.
O A syllable is a word part with one vowel sound . It can have one or more
vowel letters but only one vowel sound.
O Words like
bread (5 letters) and
pot (3 letters) look different but both have
one syllable because they only have one vowel sound.
Examples: a rm, j u mp, f ee d, br ea d
