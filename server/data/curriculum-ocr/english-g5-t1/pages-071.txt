Writing a Narrative Text in the First Person
1. First Person :Use " I ", " me ", " my "
2. Setting : Where is your story happening?
3. Goal Setting : What do you want to do?
4. Description : Use the 5 senses and other adjectives.
5. Ending : How does the story finish?
The word "narrative" comes from
"narration" or
"telling a story" .
But, don't forget that a good story is one that readers can see
in their minds (imagine) . Good writers use a lot of description to
do this.
