Let's Make a Recipe!
Main Ingredients :
 2 cups white rice
 1 cup brown lentils
 2 cups small pasta
 1½ cups cooked chickpeas
Crispy onions:
 3 large sliced onions
 1 cup vegetable oil for frying
Sauces:
 Red sauce
 Spicy tomato sauce
 Dakka sauce
Koshari is a tasty Egyptian meal with many
simple ingredients in one bowl. First , you
need to cook rice in a pot with water. In
another pot, prepare small pasta. Then , boil
lentils in water. For the red sauce, fry some
chopped garlic in oil, add tomato sauce,
vinegar, and some spices.
You also need to make crispy onions. First ,
cut them into thin slices. Next , fry them in
oil until they turn brown and crunchy. After
that , put the rice in a bowl and add the
pasta and lentils on top. Then , add some
chickpeas.
Finally , pour the red sauce all over it, and
put the crispy onions on top. There are two
more sauces you can add for more flavor .
One is a spicy tomato sauce, and the other is
Dakka which is made with garlic and vinegar.
This mix of foods makes Koshari very yummy.
Koshari is a traditional Egyptian food.
