Pronunciation
1 Read and learn.
A trigraph is a group of three letters that represents a single sound.
The trigraph “ dge ” sounds like " j ".
Example: “bri dge ” is pronounced "bri j "
The trigraph " tch " sounds like " ch "
Example: "ma tch " is pronounced "ma ch "
The trigraph " igh " sounds like " i "
Example: "r igh t" is pronounced "r i te"
