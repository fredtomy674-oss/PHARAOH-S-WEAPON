Tip!
When you write a non-fiction text, ask yourself:
Is the information true?
Did I make anything up?
Is my source reliable?
Non-Fiction writing should include:
An introductory sentence, like
"My neighbor, Selim, is a doctor at our local
hospital. "
Details that are all true, like
"He wears a white coat and carries a
stethoscope around his neck."
Adjectives, like
"white", "big", "kind".
A closing sentence, like
"The work is tiring, but he’s proud to be a doctor."
