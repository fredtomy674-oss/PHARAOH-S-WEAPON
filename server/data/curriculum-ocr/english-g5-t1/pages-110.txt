Glossary
Community
Restaurant A place where people go to eat food
Supermarket A large store where people buy
groceries
Club A place where people play sports and
socialize
Bakery A place where bread, cakes, and
pastries are made
Coffee shop A place to relax and drink coffee
Theme park A place with rides and games for fun
Village A small community or group of houses
Natural Resources
Minerals Natural substances found in the earth
Cotton A soft fiber used to make cloth
Fossil fuel Fuels like coal and oil formed from
ancient remains
Soil The top layer of earth in which plants
grow
Electricity Power used to run machines and lights
Sunlight Light from the sun, important for life
Wind Moving air that can be used for energy
Weather
Sandstorm A severe wind that carries sand through
the air
Thunderstorm A storm with lightning and thunder
Flood A large amount of water covering dry
land
Drought A long time without rain
Wildfire A fire that spreads quickly in forests
Heat wave A period of extremely hot weather
Khamsin A hot, dry, and dusty wind in Egypt
Project Types
Flyer A short printed paper used to give
information or advertise something
Menu A list of food and drinks you can choose
from at a restaurant or coffee shop
Poster A large printed paper used to display
information visually
Collage A piece of art made by sticking pictures
together
