7 Read and put the events in order.
Billie felt afraid when she first stayed alone in the Everglades.
Billie went to the Everglades to listen to the Earth, as the elders told her.
Billie learned how to survive by watching the animals and learned how to catch fish
and make fire.
Billie told the elders that she didn't believe that the Earth could talk.
Billie started to listen to the world around her and pay attention to nature.
Billie realized that the Earth talks through nature, like the wind and animals, not with
words.
