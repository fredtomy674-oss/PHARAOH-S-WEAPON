Prepositions of Time
We use “at’’ with
clock times ,
mealtimes , and some
time phrases .
Examples:
O I get up at 6 a.m. every day.
O The coffee shop is very busy at lunchtime .
O At night , the village is calm and quiet.
We use “in” with parts of the day , months , seasons , and years .
Examples:
O I liked visiting my uncle in the afternoons .
O The weather is hot in summer .
O I moved to Alexandria in 2020 .
O In December , the weather is cold.
We use “on” with
days and
dates .
Examples:
O On Saturdays , everyone gathers in the community center.
O My birthday is on June 8 th .
