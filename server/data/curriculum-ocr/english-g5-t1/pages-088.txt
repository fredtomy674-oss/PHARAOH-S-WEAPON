Writing Object-Perspective Narratives
1. First Person :Use " I ", " me ", " my " with number 2 .
2. Non-living thing : What are you?
(Water? Copper? Oil?)
3. Description : Use the 5 senses and other adjectives.
4. Ending : Why are you important?
This type of writing where non-human things like objects,
animals, or natural resources
"speak" improves your creativity,
and improves your
descriptive writing skills.
Example: I Am Gold
I sleep deep under the earth, hidden and quiet. People search for me, dream
about me, and admire me. I sit in the crowns of kings and the rings of queens.
I am soft and beautiful. I am treasured because I am valuable. I am gold.
Choose another natural resource. Write your own Object-Perspective
Narrative about it, write (40-60) words.
