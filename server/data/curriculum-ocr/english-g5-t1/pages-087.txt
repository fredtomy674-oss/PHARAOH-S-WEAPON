I Am Water
I am water. You might see me in a
glass, a river, a cloud, or a tear .
I can be quiet and still, or wild
and crashing. I sleep in lakes and
dance in the rain.
People drink me when they're
thirsty, bathe in me when they're
dirty and cry me when they're hurt. Plants need me, animals search
for me, and without me—life would stop.
Sometimes, I help. I clean. I cool. I grow things.
Sometimes, I destroy . I flood. I freeze. I fall too hard.
But I never stop moving. I am always part of something greater—
clouds, oceans, even sweat .
I am water. I have no shape, but I can shape the world.
