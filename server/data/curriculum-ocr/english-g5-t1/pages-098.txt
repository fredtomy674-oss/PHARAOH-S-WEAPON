She watched the animals closely and saw how they survived . The alligators
waited patiently for their food instead of wasting energy. The turtles hid
in their shells when danger was near. The birds warned each other when
an animal came to attack . Billie realized that nature had its own way of
teaching. She learned how to catch fish, make a fire, and find shelter, just
like the animals did.
