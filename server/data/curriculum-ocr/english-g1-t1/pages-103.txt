Learning Outcomes
Identify animal names and habitats of pets and
jungle animals.
Describe jungle animals using counting-related
sentences.
Identify the formation, sound, and words that
begin with the letters Bb, Ll, Uu, and Jj.
Follow simple instructions using prepositions.
Understand simple positional sentences.
Trace and write letters Bb, Ll, Uu, and Jj in their
uppercase and lowercase forms.
