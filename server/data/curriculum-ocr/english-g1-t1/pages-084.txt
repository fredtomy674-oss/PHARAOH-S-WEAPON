Learning Outcomes
Name farm animals and the food they give using
complete sentences.
Answer simple questions related to counting and
describing animals.
Identify the formation, sound and words that begin with
the letters Mm, Gg, Oo, and Ff.
Describe animal sizes using opposite words.
Recognize the formation of numbers 6 to 10 and
match them with their correct values.
Trace and write letters Mm, Gg, Oo, and Ff in their
uppercase and lowercase forms.
