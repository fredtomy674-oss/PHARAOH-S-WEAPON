Determinative:
The Ancient Egyptian used the Determinative, which is a hieroglyphic sign
that comes at the end of the word to clarify its meaning, and it does not
represent a phonetic value (that is, it is not pronounced), and it was
illustrated in red in some words:
Seated man, sometimes used as A determinative, to
indicate that the word is masculine.
Seated woman, sometimes used as A determi-
native, to indicate that the word is feminine.
