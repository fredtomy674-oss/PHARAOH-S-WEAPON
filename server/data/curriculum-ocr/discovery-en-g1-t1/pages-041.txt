EGYPTIAN TRADITIONS
DIRECTIONS: Read the information to discover some
Egyptian traditions.

SONGS: One traditional song is about the life of Beni
Helal. The singer plays the Rababa.

FOOD: Koshari is a traditional Egyptian dish.
It is made with rice, lentils, and tomato sauce.
Another traditional dish is Ful medames. It is
made with soft beans.

CLOTHING: Traditional costumes depend on where
people live. Many years ago, women in Alexandria
and other regions used to wear melaya laf. Men used
to wear galabiyas, trousers, and koftans.
