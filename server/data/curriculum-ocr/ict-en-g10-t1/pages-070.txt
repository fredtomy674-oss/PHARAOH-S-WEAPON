(2) Data amount [bits] per second = Sampling frequency × Quantization bit depth × Number of channels
Therefore, the amount of data per second is 44,100 [Hz] × 16 [bits] × 2 [channels] = 1,411,200 [bits].
Since 1 B = 8 bits, 1,411,200 [bits] ÷ 8 = 176,400 [B]
1 KB = 1,000 B, so 176,400 [B] ÷ 1,000 = 176.4 ≈ 176 [KB] 176 KB
(3) 44.1 kHz = 44.1 × 1,000 = 44,100 Hz, and since 2 minutes = 120 seconds, the data amount per minute is
44,100 [Hz] × 24 [bits] × 2 [channels] × 60 [seconds] = 127,008,000 [bits]
Since 1 B = 8 bits, 127,008,000 [bits] ÷ 8 = 15,876,000 [B].
1 KB = 1,000 B, 1 GB = 1,000 MB, therefore,
15,876,000 [B] ÷ 1,000 ÷ 1,000 = 15.876 [MB] ≈ 16 [MB] 16 MB
