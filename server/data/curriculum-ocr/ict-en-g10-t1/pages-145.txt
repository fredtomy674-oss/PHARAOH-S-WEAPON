Methods of Referencing in Spreadsheet Software
(1) (1Relative reference) : A method of referencing where the position of the referenced cell changes
automatically.
(2) (2Absolute reference) : A method of referencing that keeps the position of the row or column of
the referred cell consistently fixed.
By adding “(3$),” it becomes an absolute reference.
<Example> If you copy the formula as “$C$3,” every formula will be able
to refer to cell C3.
Simulations
(1) (4Simulation) : Manipulating a model in order to predict phenomena or events. Even when it is
difficult to use actual objects, predictions can be made through simulations.
