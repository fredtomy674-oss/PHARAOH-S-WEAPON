7-3 Logic Circuits
Logic Circuits
(1) (1Logical operations) : Operations performed using combinations of the numbers 0 and 1. In
computers, “1” is processed as true and “0” as false.
(2) (2Logic circuit) : A circuit designed to perform logical operations.
(3) (3Truth table) : A table that shows all possible combinations of inputs and outputs for a logic
circuit.
(4) (4AND gate (logical conjunction circuit)) : A circuit that outputs 1 only when all inputs are 1.
(5) (5OR gate (logical disjunction circuit)) : A circuit that outputs 1 if at least one of the inputs is 1.
(6) (6NOT circuit (inverter circuit)) : A circuit that outputs the opposite result of the input.
Half Adder Circuit and Full Adder Circuit
(1) (7Half adder circuit) : A circuit representing the addition of single-digit numbers, composed of
AND, OR, and NOT gates.
(2) (8Full adder circuit) : A circuit that considers carry from the lower bit and carry to the higher bit.
