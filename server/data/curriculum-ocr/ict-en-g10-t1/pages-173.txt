Explanation
(1) In programming, the function is defined as shown below.

Therefore, the programs that fit in blanks A and B are as follows.

“circle”.
Therefore, when row [1] is executed, it is 5 × 5 × 3.14 = 78.5, so 78.5 is displayed.
