Organizing and Classifying Information
(16LATCH method) : A principle for organizing information and presenting it in a way that is easy for
users to understand. Information is organized and classified according to the
following five criteria.
• Location : Classification based on physical location.
• Alphabet : Classification based on alphabetical or syllabary order.
• Time : A classification based on the sequence of events in terms of time.
• Category : A classification based on the differences between things, used as a criterion to
distinguish realms.
• Hierarchy : Classification based on quantitative changes such as size, level, etc.
