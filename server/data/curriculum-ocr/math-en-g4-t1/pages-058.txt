Elapsed Time

Learning Targets

Error Analysis Analyze the student’s work and answer. Identify what the student did correctly and incorrectly, and then try to solve the problem correctly.

How many minutes does the drive take?
Student’s work: 2 x 6 = 12 12 + 15 = 27 It took 27 minutes.

What did the student do correctly?
What did the student do incorrectly? Why do you think they made this error?
