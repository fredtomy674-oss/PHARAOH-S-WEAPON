Learning Target

ACCESS
Error Analysis Analyze the student’s work and answer that follow. Identify what the student did correctly and incorrectly, and then try to solve the problem correctly.
Amir is a myrmecologist who found a large fire ant mound. He placed string around the outside of the mound in a rectangular shape so that he could study the mound safely. The width of the rectangle was 8 meters and the length of the rectangle was 12 meters. How many square meters are roped off to study?
Student’s Solution:
There are 40 meters of ground to study. 8 + 8 + 12 + 12 = 40.
