ACTIVITY
Creating Big Numbers

Practice creating, reading, and comparing really big numbers.
1. Place all digit cards face down in the middle and mix them up.
2. One person in the group turns over a digit card. Each player independently decides which place value to put the digit in and records the digit in their own place value chart.
3. The next student turns over a card. Each player independently decides which place value to put the digit in and records the digit in their own place value chart.
4. Repeat until all spaces in the row have been filled. Then, players compare their numerals. The player with the greatest numeral wins a point. There is room to play 5 rounds.
