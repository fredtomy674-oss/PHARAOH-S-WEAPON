Many Forms to Write Numbers

Learning Target
• I can write numerals in standard, word, and expanded forms.

ACCESS
Same or Different? Omar and Mariam are amateur myrmecologists, scientists who study ants. They are conducting research to find out if ant populations are declining. Part of their research involves counting the number of ants that live in one anthill. Every day they count ants.

At the end of the month, Omar recorded the number of ants he had counted as follows:
1,467,303,221
Mariam recorded her count in this way:
1,000,000,000 + 400,000,000 + 60,000,000 + 7,000,000
+ 300,000 + 3,000 + 200 + 20 + 1
