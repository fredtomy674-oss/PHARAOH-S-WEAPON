Learning Target

ACCESS
Which Does Not Belong? Solve the problems. Then, think about which problem does not belong in the set. Highlight or circle the problem you think does not belong and explain your thinking.

1. 6 x 4 - 4 = _________
2. 100 - 80 x 1 = _________
3. 60 + 20 - 50 = _________
4. 2,356 - 2,336 = _________

BUILD
Find the Answer Work with your partner to solve each problem. Locate the correct answer and write the equation under it. If the answer is not listed, rewrite the problem under “Other.”
