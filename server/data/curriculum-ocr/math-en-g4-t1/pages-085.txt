Learning Targets
• I can explain the Commutative Property of Multiplication.

ACCESS
Toy Car Number Talk Solve the problem. Give the teacher a Thumbs-Up when you are finished.
Ahmed has 48 toy cars and wants to display them in his room. He wants to arrange them in equal rows and equal columns. How can he display his cars? Draw your solution.

BUILD
Exploring the Commutative Property of Multiplication Turn and talk about what you remember about the Commutative Property of Multiplication.
Write a definition for the Commutative Property of Multiplication in your own words. Include an example.

HANDS-ON ACTIVITY
Arrays and the Commutative Property
1. Take out your digit cards (1–9). Choose 2 digit cards. Draw an array using the numbers you chose as factors (numbers of rows and columns). Then, use the same numbers to draw a new array. Write an equation for your arrays using the Commutative Property of Multiplication.
