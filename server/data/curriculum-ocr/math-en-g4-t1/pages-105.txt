UNIT 6
CONCEPT 2
Understanding Multiples

LESSON 6
Relationships between Factors and Multiples

Learning Targets
• I can explain the relationship between factors and multiples.
• I can determine if a number is a factor or a multiple of another number.

ACCESS
Multiples Riddles Read each riddle and solve. There may be more than one answer.

1. The number is an odd number. It is a multiple of 3 and 5. It is greater than 20. What number is it?

2. The number is an even number. It is a multiple of 4 and 8. It is between 10 and 20. What number is it?

3. The number is an even number. It is a multiple of 3, 4, and 6. What number is it?

BUILD
Making Connections Think about the relationships between the numbers in each group. Write at least two sentences describing what you notice. Be ready to share your thinking.

1. 3, 6, and 12

2. 4, 8, 16, and 24
