UNIT 8
CONCEPT 1
Order of Operations

LESSON 2
The Order of Operations and Story Problems

Learning Targets
• I can use the order of operations to solve problems with multiple operations.
• I can write and solve an equation to represent what is happening in a multistep story problem.

ACCESS
Number Talk Solve the problems. Then, rewrite each problem more efficiently.
1. 67 + 67 + 67 + 67 + 67 - 15 = _________________________________
2. 568 + 78 - 4 - 4 - 4 - 4 - 4 - 4 - 4 - 4 = _________________________________

BUILD
The Order of Operations and Story Problems Use numbers and symbols to represent what is happening in each problem, and then solve. Remember the order of operations.
1. Abdullah loves collecting stamps. He received 246 stamps for his birthday. He kept 25 of the stamps and now he wants to give the rest to 6 of his friends. How many stamps will each friend get if they share them equally?
2. Maha walked 14 kilometers every day for 2 weeks. The next week she walked 56 kilometers. How many kilometers did she walk over those 3 weeks?
