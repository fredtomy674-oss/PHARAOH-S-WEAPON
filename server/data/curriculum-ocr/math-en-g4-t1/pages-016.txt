HANDS-ON
ACTIVITY
Creating the Greatest

Play Creating the Greatest with a partner to practice creating numbers and
writing them in multiple forms.
• Combine decks of digit cards with your partner. Place the cards face down
in a pile and mix them up.
• Turn over 10 digit cards and record the numbers in the order in which you
turned them over.
• Rearrange the 10 cards to create the greatest numeral.
• Record the numeral in standard form, word form, and expanded form.
