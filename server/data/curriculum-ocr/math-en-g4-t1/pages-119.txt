Dividing by 1-Digit Divisors

LESSON 6
Exploring Remainders
Learning Targets
• I can identify the dividend, divisor, and quotient of a
division problem.
• I can solve division problems.
• I can explain what a remainder represents in a division problem.

ACCESS
Compare and Connect Read each problem with a partner. Answer each problem.
1. There are 8 teams playing soccer. There are 9 students on each team. How
many students are there in all?

2. There are 72 students on the field. They want to make teams with 9 students
on each team. How many teams will they be able to make?

3. There are 72 students on the field. They want to split into 8 teams. How many
students will be on each team?

4. How are the problems alike? How are they different?
