Can Ants Swim?
Some ants can swim, and some drown. It depends on the species. Some researchers took 35 species of tropical ants and dropped them into the water. Some of them were amazing swimmers, especially trap jaw ants. One species could swim an amazing 16–17 cm per second. That means in a minute the ant could swim 1,020 cm or approximately 10 meters!

Fire ants cannot swim, but they can join their legs to form a circular raft to survive floods. Sometimes, the entire ant colony will join legs to survive.
