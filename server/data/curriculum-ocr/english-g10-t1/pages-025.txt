It was a difficult week for Sama. Her grades were
 
slipping
, and she felt
 
overwhelmed
 
by
all her schoolwork. On Thursday afternoon, she sat by her window,

Then, there was a knock at the door. It was Mrs. Aya, the friendly neighbor who lived

“I baked some fresh bread this morning,” Mrs. Aya said warmly. “I thought you might like
some.”
The smell was
 
irresistible
, wrapping around Sama like a gentle hug. Sama smiled for the
first time in days and invited her in for tea. They sat together, chatting about small things-

Slowly, Sama felt her worries began to
 
fade
.
Before leaving, Mrs. Aya said, “Sometimes, the
smallest kindness can make a big difference.”
Sama kept thinking about those words. She
decided to help her younger cousin with
homework that evening. Seeing her cousin’s
face

filled her with
 
optimism
.
She realized that kindness doesn’t just make
others feel better; it can change your own
heart, too.
