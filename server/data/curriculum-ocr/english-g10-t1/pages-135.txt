Values in this Chapter

•
 
Triumph through suffering: Santiago kills the marlin but at
great cost.

•
 
Respect for the noble opponent: He asks the fish’s

forgiveness.
•
 
Dignity in victory: His pride is mixed with sorrow.
•
 
Man and nature: Victory binds them together forever.

Idioms/Expressions

•
 
“Forgive me, fish” – Respect in conquest.

• “We fought well” – Expression of fair struggle.

• “Joined forever” – Connection between man and the sea.
