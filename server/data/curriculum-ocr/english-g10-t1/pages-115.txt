CHAPTER 2

Preparing for the Great Day

In the early morning, before the sun rose, Santiago woke up. The
sky was dark and full of stars. He loved this hour, when the village
was quiet and the sea whispered in the distance. He put on his old
trousers, picked up his coil of lines, and stepped outside his small
hut.
The boy, Manolin, was already waiting for him. He carried food
wrapped in paper—a gift for Santiago. “Here, old man. Some black
beans, rice, and a little coffee,” he said proudly.
