6
 
Advice Blog Post: Students write a blog post of (120 - 150) words on “How to Avoid
Fake News in Egypt.” They must use at least two examples of each conditional (zero,
first, second) to give advice to Egyptian teens. Encourage use of vocabulary like
“scam,” “reliable,” and “misinformation.”

Example:

• If you see a sensational headline, you feel curious.
• If you check sources, you will avoid scams.
