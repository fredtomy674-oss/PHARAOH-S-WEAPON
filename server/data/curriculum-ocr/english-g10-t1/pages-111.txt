The Old Man and His Luck

That evening, the boy sat with him outside the hut. They

shared a simple meal of black beans, rice, and a little fried fish.

The boy had brought it as a gift from his own home. Santiago
ate slowly, gratefully, for food was energy, and tomorrow he
would need it.
“Tell me about baseball,” Santiago said suddenly. Baseball

was one of the old man’s greatest loves. “How are the Yankees

playing this season?”

“The Yankees are strong,” Manolin replied eagerly. “Joe

DiMaggio is playing well. He is always the hero.”
