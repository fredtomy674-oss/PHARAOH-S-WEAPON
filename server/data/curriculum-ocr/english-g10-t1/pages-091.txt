Writing Task

4
 
Write your own CV. Include all relevant sections and make sure to:

• use action verbs to describe your experience
• keep descriptions clear and concise
• include relevant skills and achievements
• proofread for grammar and spelling mistakes
• format it professionally

Checklist:

• Personal information (no unnecessary details)
• Personal statement (2-3 sentences)
• Education (reverse chronological order)
• Work experience (with action verbs)
• Skills (technical and soft skills)
• Proper formatting and spelling
