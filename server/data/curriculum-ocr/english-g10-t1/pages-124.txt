Values in this Chapter

•
 
Courage in the face of struggle: Santiago meets the marlin with
calm strength.

•
 
Patience and timing: He waits for the fish to take the bait fully.
•
 
Endurance: His fight begins with quiet determination.
•
 
Respect for the opponent: Santiago calls the fish “brother.”

Idioms/Expressions

•
 
“Take it, big one” – Encouragement to embrace a challenge fully.

•
 
“Hooked” – Caught firmly, with no escape.
•
 
“My brother, the fish” – Expression of respect even for an enemy.
•
 
“Cut into his hands” – Symbol of sacrifice through pain.

•
 
“May the best of us win” – Fairness in a struggle.
