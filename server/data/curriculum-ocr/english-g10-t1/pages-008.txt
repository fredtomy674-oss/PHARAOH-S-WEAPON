Writing

6
 
Topic:
 
A Strange Day in My Life

Instructions:

Write a short narrative paragraph of about (120 - 150) words using Past Simple and Past
Continuous. Describe what happened, what you were doing, and how the day ended.
Use time expressions like while, when, during, suddenly, etc.

Example Opening:

"Last Friday was a day I’ll never forget. I was walking to school when I saw a cat sitting on
top of a car. Suddenly, it jumped and landed right in front of me..."
