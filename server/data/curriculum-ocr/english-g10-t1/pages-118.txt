Chapter 2

By noon, the heat was strong, and sweat ran down his back. But
Santiago did not complain. He kept his eyes on the sea, his hands
ready, his heart steady. He was prepared for the great test.
Far out, a bird circled over the water. Santiago watched it carefully.

“Where the bird circles, there are fish,” he said aloud. His eyes

followed the movement, and deep inside, he knew that soon, his
moment would come.
