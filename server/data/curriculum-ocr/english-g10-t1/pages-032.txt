Read the following passage.

My name is Radwan, and I work as a firefighter in Alexandria. Many people think our
job is just putting out fires, but it’s much more than that. We
 
rescue
 
people from car
accidents, help during floods, and sometimes save animals trapped in dangerous places.
The truth is, our work can be
 
risky
. I have entered burning buildings where smoke made it
hard to see and breathe. I’ve stood on shaking ladders in the middle of the night, with the
heat almost burning my skin. Sadly, I have also seen lives lost before we could reach them.
These moments stay with me forever.
Yet, we do it because we believe in
 
obligation
 
– the duty to help each other, no matter
the cost. In every
 
emergency
, we must act quickly, stay calm, and
 
trust
 
our training. We
don’t think about fear in those moments; we think about the people waiting for us.
I am proud to serve my community. I know that my work saves lives, and that is worth every
risk. When you help others, you are not just doing a job – you are
 
protecting
 
hope itself.
