Indefinite article: A/an
Indefinite article: A/an
• Use "a" before singular countable
nouns starting with a constant sound
(a man, a university, a European
team).
• Use "an" before singular countable
nouns starting with a vowel sound
(an egg, an orange, an honest woman).
• Use a or an to mean one of many
things.
• Use a or an to refer to something
mentioned for the first time.

Definite article: The
Definite article: The
• Use the with both singular and plural
nouns.
• Use the when there’s only one of
something; when you are talking about
one specific example of something
(the earth, the world, the news, the
environment …).
• Use the to refer to something for the
second time. - Malak has baked a cake.
The cake is really delicious.
• The is used before the names of
countries preceded by Republic/
Kingdom/ States, etc.
(The Arab Republic of Egypt/ The
Kingdom of Saudi Arabia / The United
States of America, etc.)

Note:
Note: We say: He lives in Egypt /the
Arab Republic of Egypt. Not: He lives in
the Egypt.
• Use the before (musical instruments –
mountains – superlatives).
