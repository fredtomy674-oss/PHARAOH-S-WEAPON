Algebraic Expressions from Written Expressions and Real-World Scenarios
To write algebraic expressions, assign a variable to the unknown number. Use parentheses when
a sum or difference is multiplied. For example, "Four times the sum of a number and seven,"
translates to 4(x + 7), while "the sum of four times a number and seven" is 4x + 7.

Less than and more than are key words for switching the order of the terms. For example, a
number less than ten translates to "10 - x", while a number less ten is "x - 10".
