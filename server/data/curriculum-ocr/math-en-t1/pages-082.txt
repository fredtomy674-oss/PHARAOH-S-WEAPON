Suppose a student chose a ride and said that for her ride, the number of tickets, $t$, she would
need to go on her ride $r$ times could be represented by $t = 5r$.

Which ride did she choose? How can you use her equation to find how many tickets she would
need if she wanted to go on this ride 15 times?

The student says that she now understands why she needs to learn about dependent and independent
variables. She can see in the equation $t = 5r$, that the value of $r$ depends on the value of $t$.

Explain whether she is correct.
