Sets of Numbers When placing numbers
into groupings for rational, integer, natural,
or counting numbers, it is important to place
the number in its best set. Subsets are within
sets. Example: Counting numbers includes
the number 2 even though it is also a natural,
integer and rational number. Being that
counting numbers' set is within the natural set
and natural number set is within the integer set
and the integer set is within the rational set.
