COMPARING EGYPT'S CLIMATES
Read the story to learn about climates in Egypt.

"My favorite time of the year is winter in Alexandria because the temperatures always feel
a little cooler," said Rashad. "Even though it is rainy, I
like the break from the hot and humid summer."

"One time we went to Luxor to visit family in the
winter," replied Zeina. "But it was still so hot, and it had
not rained in months. I was happy to get back home."

"That is so strange. Luxor and Alexandria are both in
Egypt. I wonder why the weather was so different. Is it
always like that?" asked Rashad.

"I think so," said Zeina. "Luxor has a desert climate. It is
hot and dry. But I still wonder if it ever rains there."
