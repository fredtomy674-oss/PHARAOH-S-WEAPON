MEETING PEOPLE'S NEEDS

Read the passage. Circle the types of transportation you have used to connect with other people.

Each form of transportation provides a unique set of advantages and disadvantages, making it ideal for different circumstances.

Bicycles are very useful for short journeys in good weather.

Automobiles can carry people quickly and efficiently within their communities and regions, while trucks and trains can carry heavy cargo.

Ships are ideal for carrying cargo over the ocean.

Airplanes are useful for traveling very long distances or over oceans quickly.

People work together and use many types of transportation to support a community.
