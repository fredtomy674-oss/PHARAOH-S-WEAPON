WHAT HAPPENS TO THE FOOD YOU EAT?

Read the text.

The body has a process that changes the food we eat into simpler parts. Once food is
broken down, the body absorbs it to get energy.

Inside your stomach, muscles churn acid to further
break down the food. Digested food moves to the
intestines to continue the digestion process. The
nutrients are carried away in the blood, giving all parts
of the human body energy to run.
