Punctuation Tips
• We use capital letters at the beginning of the sentence.
Example: T he Pyramids of Giza were built a long time ago in Egypt.
• We use periods at the end of sentences, and question marks
at the end of questions.
Examples: - The Nile River is the longest river in the world .
- When was the Grand Egyptian Museum built ?
