We Share, We Care We Share, We Care
Salma and Youssef had a project at school. At first, they couldn't
agree on what to do. Then, they started to share ideas. Salma
wrote, and Youssef drew. When Salma felt tired, Youssef helped her.
They showed care and worked well together. Finally, they finished
the project. Their teacher was happy and said, “Good job!”
