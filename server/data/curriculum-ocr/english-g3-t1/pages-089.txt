Action Verbs
Action verbs are words that show what someone does. They
tell us about an action.
Example:
• Salma helps Youssef with the project.
• They solve the problem together.
• Youssef finds a good idea.
• They work as a team.
• They plan their project first.
• Salma fixes the poster.
• They agree on what to do.
