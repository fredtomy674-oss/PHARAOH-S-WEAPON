Phonics Focus: : Soft "g" = /d / sound
" g " usually makes a soft sound / d / when it is followed by (e,i, or y)
Examples: giraffe, Egypt, page, huge

1. _ i r a f f e 2. E _ y p t 3. p a _ e 4. h u _ e

1. game - giraffe - goat
