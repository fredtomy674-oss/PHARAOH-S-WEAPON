Present Simple Tense for Routines
Examples
• I wake up at 7 a.m.
• He goes to school at 8 a.m.
• We brush our teeth in the morning.
• She does her homework every day.
• I make my bed when I wake up.
Subject Present Tense Example
I play I play football.
You play You play with your friends.
He plays He plays in the park.
She plays She plays the piano.
It plays It plays in the garden.
We play We play a game together.
They play They play after school.
• Add “-s” to the verb when using he , she , or it .
• If the verb ends in ch , sh , x , ss , or o , add – es with he / she / it .
• If a verb ends in a consonant + y , change the y to i and add
– es with he / she / it .
• If the verb ends in a vowel + y , add - s. with he / she / it .
