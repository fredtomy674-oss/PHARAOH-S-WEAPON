Let’s Learn Together! Let’s Learn Together!
Speaking
• Use common greetings in everyday
conversations.
• Use simple phrases for everyday
classroom use.
• Describe daily routines using simple
sentences and time expressions.
• Use simple sentences to describe
oneself and others.
• Pronounce the digraph 'wh' in common
English words correctly.
Listening
• Respond appropriately to common
greetings in a variety of classroom
contexts.
• Follow basic classroom instructions
during teacher-led activities.
• Identify simple descriptive sentences
about people in short listening texts.
• Recognize the digraph 'wh' and
related words during structured
listening tasks.
Reading
• Read simple sentences and short
dialogs about greetings, describing
others, and daily routines.
• Interpret phrases related to basic
classroom rules.
Writing
• Write full sentences describing others
and daily routines using the present
simple tense.
