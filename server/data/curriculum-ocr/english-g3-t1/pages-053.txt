Phonics Focus: Digraph "ck"=/k/ Sound
" ck " refers to a spelling rule for the / k / sound, especially at the end of words .
The letters " ck " together make one / k / sound (like in "cat" or "kite") .
/ ck / sounds like k
Examples:
du ck so ck ba ck ne ck tru ck
