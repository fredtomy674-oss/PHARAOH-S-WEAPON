Samy and the Toy Car Samy and the Toy Car
1 Read then circle the prepositions of place in the story
One day, Samy was playing in his room when his toy car rolled under the
couch. He looked for it behind the curtain and in front of the TV, but it was
gone. Then, he checked between the two chairs. Samy found the car but
it was stuck . His sister came to help. She used a stick to pull it out from
under the couch. Samy was so happy! He put the toy car on the table
and smiled.
