1 Read the following story
One sunny day, Sara went to the park because she was bored. She drew
a picture with her pencil. She was happy and smiled at her friend. Then she fell
and hurt her knee. Sara felt sad and scared. Her friend helped her stand up.
Sara said, “Thank you”, and smiled again. Now she was tired, but she felt better.
