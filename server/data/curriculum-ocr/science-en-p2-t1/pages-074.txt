Absorption of light by Chlorophyll

Activity 1 Practical

Materials and Tools Used :
• Spinach leaves. • Ethyl alcohol (ethanol). • Filter papers.
• Test tube. • Light bulb. • Porcelain mortar and pestle (Figure 6).
• Coloured transparent sheets.

Steps :
1 Cut some spinach leaves and place them in the mortar.
2 Add enough alcohol to the spinach leaves to cover them.
3 Crush the leaves in the mortar using the pestle, till a green mixture is formed.
Why does the mixture become green in colour ? ....................................
4 Use filter paper to separate the clear green solution in the test tube.
5 Place a red transparent sheet between the light bulb and the test tube containing the green solution (Figure 8) to allow only red light to pass through.
Does the red light pass through the green solution ? ....................................
6 Repeat step 5 one time using a blue transparent sheet (Figure 9) and another time using a green transparent sheet (Figure 10).
What do you observe ? ....................................

It is clear from the previous observations that the clear chlorophyll solution only transmits the green light from white light and absorbs both red light and blue light, whose energy is used in the photosynthetic reactions that take place in the grana to convert light energy into chemical energy that is stored in glucose.
