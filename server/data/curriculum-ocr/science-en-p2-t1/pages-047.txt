From your previous study, you learned the difference
between physical changes and chemical changes:
- A physical change is a change in the state or
shape of a substance without changing its chemical
composition,such as the melting of ice (Figure 1).
- Chemical change is a change that results in new
substances with different chemical properties
from those of the original substances, such as
the combustion of wood (Figure 2). This change
occurs when the original substances react
chemically to make new compounds.
