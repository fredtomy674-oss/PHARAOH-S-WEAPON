Conclusion :
The amount of the change in temperature of different masses of the same substance varies
when they gain or lose the same quantity of the thermal energy.
The change in temperature of the substance increases as its mass decreases, where
the thermal energy gained by substance is distributed over fewer particles, so the average
kinetic energy increases by a greater extent.
