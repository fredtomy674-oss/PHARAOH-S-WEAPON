The particle model of matter

Lets imagine ourselves on a journey inside matter,
wandering among tiny units called particles.
Imagine particles as tiny spheres that make up everything,
such as water, air, and blocks of metals (Figure 7).
What does make the properties of matter vary
depending on its physical state?
We can explain many of the physical properties of matter
in terms of the behavior of its particles.
