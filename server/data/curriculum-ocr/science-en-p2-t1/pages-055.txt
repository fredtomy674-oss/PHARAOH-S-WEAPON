Lesson Objectives :
By the end of the lesson, students should be able to:
1 Recognize the concept of chemical equations.
2 Deduce the law of conservation of mass (conservation of matter).
3 Determine the relation between the law of conservation of mass and
balancing of a chemical equation.
4 List the steps for balancing a chemical equation.
5 Balance chemical equations.
6 Design models at the molecular level for some molecules during
a chemical reaction.
