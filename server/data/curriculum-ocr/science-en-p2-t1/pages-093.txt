3 Transform movement

• The horizontal movement of tectonic plates that
are parallel to each other (side by side) is known
as transform (Lateral sliding) movement (Figure 7).
and the areas where the plates move are called
transform boundaries.

The transform movement resulted in the formation
of long faults such as San Andreas fault (Figure 8).
