Energy and Metabolism
Living organisms need energy to carry out all the vital processes necessary for survival,
including growth and movement. This is achieved through a series of chemical reactions that
take place in the body's cells.
These reactions include anabolism (building up) and catabolism (breaking down) processes,
known as metabolism.

Anabolism (Building up processes)
Processes in which the energy is consumed in building a large, complex molecule from
many simple molecules (Figure 5), such as the formation of proteins from amino acids
in the cytoplasm.

Catabolism (Breaking down processes)
Processes in which the energy is released by breaking down a large molecule that has been built
before into simpler molecules (Figure 6), such as breaking down of glucose molecules during
cellular respiration and breaking down of protein molecules.
