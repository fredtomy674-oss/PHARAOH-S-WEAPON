Cross-Cutting Concepts :
• Cause and effect.

Lesson Objectives :
By the end of the lesson, the student should be able to :
1 Classify materials into solids, liquids and gases.
2 Compare the physical properties of matter in its fundamental states.
3 Use particle theory to describe the behavior of matter in its solid, liquid and gaseous states.
4 Explain some physical properties of matter in different states in terms of particle theory.
5 Appreciate the role of scientists in discovering the plasma state and its applications.
