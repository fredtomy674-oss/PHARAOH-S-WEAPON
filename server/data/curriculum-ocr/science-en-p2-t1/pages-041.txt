Explanation of convection currents :
• When a container of water is placed on a heat source, the water
particles at the bottom of the container gain thermal energy,
they move faster and further away from each other, resulting in
decreasing the density of the hot water, which rises to the top.
At the same time, the cold water which has higher density sinks
to the bottom to replace the hot water.
Heat transfer by this method is known as convection currents,
which occurs with the movement (the currents) of liquid and
gaseous (fluids) particles. (Figure 16).
