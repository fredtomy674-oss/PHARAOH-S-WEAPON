Scientific Skills Interpretation
• Why is the process of breaking bonds in glucose molecules accompanied by energy
absorption, while the process of breaking down glucose is accompanied by energy release?

Cross-Cutting Concepts: Cause and Effect
Breaking down the nutrients such as glucose involves breaking bonds and forming new
bonds (Cause), consequently it is accompanied by the formation of new products and
the release of energy (Effect).
