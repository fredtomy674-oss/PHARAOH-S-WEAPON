The Importance of Carbohydrates
• There are many ways in which living organisms benefit from carbohydrates, as follows:
1 They form a source of energy for the body.
2 They support the brain functions.
3 Plant cell walls are made of cellulose.
4 They participate in forming the nectar of flowers.
• Carbohydrates are used in many industries, such as making :
1 Paper from cellulose.
2 Some cosmetics from sugars and starch.
3 Medicine capsules (Figure 4).

Nutritional Awareness
► It is recommended to reduce carbohydrates found in white sugar and white flour in the meals and to rely on whole grains.

Second Fats
• Fats are organic chemical compounds that contain the same elements as carbohydrates, but provide larger amount of energy than carbohydrates.
The most important sources of fats are butter, eggs, milk, meat, many types of nuts, and vegetable oils (Figure 5).
When the body consumes more carbohydrates than its requirements, the excess is stored in the liver and muscles in form of glycogen or in the body’s cells in form of fat, which is burned when needed, such as during periods of fasting or when following a diet.

Medical Awareness
• Cholesterol is a fatty substance found with body fats, and its deposition on the inside walls of the arteries leads to their blockage and increases the risk of heart diseases (Figure 6).
• Maintaining normal cholesterol levels in the blood requires the following:
1 Reduce your intake of fats and fried foods.
2 Exercise regularly.
3 Drink plenty of water.
