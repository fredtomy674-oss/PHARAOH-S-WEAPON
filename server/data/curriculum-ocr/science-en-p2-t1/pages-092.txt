2 Convergent movement
• The movement of tectonic plates towards each other
is known as convergent movement (Figure 5)
due to the downward convection current, where
each two plates meet and one subducts (slides)
under the other. The areas where the plates
converge are called convergent boundaries.
Convergence occurs between:
- Two oceanic plates, which resulted in the formation
of new chains of volcanic islands, such as
the Japanese islands.
- Two continental plates which resulted in
the formation of the Himalayan mountain
range (Himalayas) (Figure 6).
- An oceanic plate with a continental plate :
The oceanic plate which is greater in density
subducts under the continental plate, which has
less density, this resulted in the formation of
the Andes mountain range.
