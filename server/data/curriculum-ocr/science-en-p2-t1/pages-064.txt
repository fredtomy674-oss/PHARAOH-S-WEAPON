First Carbohydrates
• Carbohydrates are organic chemical compounds
composed of carbon, hydrogen, and oxygen.
The most important sources of carbohydrates are
bread, potatoes, rice, grains, vegetables, and some
fruits such as grapes and apples (Figure 1).
They are responsible for the sweet taste of food.
