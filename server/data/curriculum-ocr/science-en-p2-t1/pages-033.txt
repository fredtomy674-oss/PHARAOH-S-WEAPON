Conclusion :
• The amount of the change in temperature of equal masses of different states of the same substance varies when they gain or lose the same quantity of thermal energy.
The increase in the temperature of a mass of ice is greater than the increase in the temperature of an equal mass of water when they gain the same quantity of thermal energy.
