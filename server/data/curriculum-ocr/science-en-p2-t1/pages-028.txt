The concept of a system
• A system is any part of the universe that is under study,
where changes in energy and matter are observed.
Each system has boundary that distinguishes it from
the surroundings.
• The matter in a system may be solid, liquid, gas, or
a mixture of them.
The system may be :
An open system in which both energy and matter are exchanged with the surroundings
or a closed system in which only energy is exchanged with the surroundings while matter
is not exchanged, or an isolated system in which neither energy nor matter is exchanged
with the surroundings.
