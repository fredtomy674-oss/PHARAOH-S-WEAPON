Cross-Cutting Concepts : Cause and Effect
When two objects with different temperatures come into contact (Cause),
Heat is transferred from the object with the higher temperature to the object with the lower temperature (Effect).

Methods of heat transfer
Heat is transferred by three methods: 1 Conduction. 2 Convection. 3 Radiation.
