A chemical reaction is defined as the process of breaking the bonds between atoms of
the reacting substances (reactants) and forming new bonds between atoms of the produced
substances (products).
A chemical reaction occurs when reactants combine, decompose, or are replaced by new
reactants to form new products. This takes place through :
1 Breaking the bonds between atoms of the reacting substances (the reactants).
2 The atoms are rearranged and combined forming new bonds to form new substances
(the products) that have different properties from the original substances (Figure 4).
