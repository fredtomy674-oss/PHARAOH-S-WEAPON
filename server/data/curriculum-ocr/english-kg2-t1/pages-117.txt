Cut, Paste, and Create!

Make Your Donkey Poster!

1-Cut out all the donkey parts carefully - the head, tail, ears, body, and hair.

2-Place the pieces on your poster paper to see where they should go.

3-Glue each part in the correct place to build your donkey.

4-Press gently to make sure all the pieces stick well.

5-Add details like grass, a sun, or a name for your donkey if you like!
