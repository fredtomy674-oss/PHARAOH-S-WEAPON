Listening and Speaking
1 “Describe and Guess” Game:
The teacher describes a room, an object, or a family member.
Example: This is where you sleep. The bedroom!
Practice
Students describe their home or family to a partner in full sentences.
“My sister is kind. We play in the garden.”
Switch roles and repeat.
