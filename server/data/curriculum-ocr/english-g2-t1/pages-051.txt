L a n g u a g e i n U s e Language in Use
This is my sister.
We use " my " when something belongs to the person who
is speaking.
Example: This is my brother .
This is your bag.
We use " your " to show that something belongs to the
person you are talking to. (one or more than one)
Example: This is your toy. These are your toys .
When something belongs to someone, we add ( 's ) to their
name to show that it is theirs.
Example: Ahmed has a car. It's Ahmed's car .
Q: Who's this?
A: This is my father. / This is your mother.
