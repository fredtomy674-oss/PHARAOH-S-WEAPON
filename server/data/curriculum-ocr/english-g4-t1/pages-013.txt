Present Simple
– We use the present simple to talk about daily habits and
routines .
– To make the affirmative form:
● We use
I , you , we , they , or a plural noun followed by the infinitive
form of the verb.
– Example: Some children play sports after school.
● We use
he , she , it , or a singular noun followed by the infinitive
form of the verb + -s/-es/-ies .
– Example: He play s football every day.
– Example: He brush es his teeth every day.
– Example: She carr ies her backpack every day.
