Tip
The Third Conditional
The Third Conditional is used to talk about unreal situations in the past.
form :
- If + past perfect (had + past participle) + would have + past participle
➡ It shows an imaginary situation and a different result that could have happened - but didn't.
If they had scored one more goal, they wouldn't have lost the match.
➡ We use it to express regret, missed chances
If I had gone, I would have met her.
➡ To express excuses
• If he had told me, I would have helped.
