Tip (2)
What are imperatives?
Imperatives are commands we give to others.
• Positive Imperatives:
They tell someone to do something.
• Example:
Brainstorm as many ideas as
possible.
• Negative Imperatives:
They tell someone Not to do
something.
• Example:
Don't judge ideas during
brainstorming.
• We use "Never" instead of "Don't"
to mean a powerful form of
prohibition:
• Example:
• Never waste your time.
