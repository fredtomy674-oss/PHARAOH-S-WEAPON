1 Read the following conversation.
What do Eman and Ali agree about?

Talking About AI
Eman : Hi Ali, have you heard about how Al is becoming really advanced these days?
Ali : Yes! I think robots with Al will be able to do many new things in the future.
They will be customized to help people in different ways.
Eman : That's so exciting! But do you think Al will ever understand emotions like humans
do?
Ali : Hmm, I'm not sure. I heard that Al has limits, and understanding feelings is one of
them. But maybe in the future, Al will be improved to recognize emotions better.
Eman : Wow, the future sounds amazing. With all this innovation, I believe many jobs will
be changed by Al.
Ali : True. Al will be used in schools, hospitals, and even at home to make life easier.
Eman : I hope people will always remember to use Al carefully and not to forget its limits.
Ali : Definitely! Even though Al is powerful, it will be controlled by humans to keep
everyone safe.
Eman : I can't wait to see what kind of robots will be created next!
Ali : Me too! The future with Al will be interesting for sure.

2 In pairs, ask and answer questions,
then switch roles.
1. How will Al be used in the future, according
to Ali and Eman?
2. How do they feel about Al being able to
understand emotions?
3. What does "Al will be customized" mean?
4. Can you think of other places or jobs where
Al will be used? Name them.

Conversation Tip:
Ask open - ended questions to keep
the conversation going.
Instead of just "yes" or "no" answers,
try questions that begin with how, why,
or what. For example:
"How do you think Al will help us in the
future?"
"Why is it important to understand
emotions?"
These questions help people share more
ideas and make conversations more
interesting.
