Experts warn that too much screen time can negatively impact mental health and
sleep routines. Many teenagers spend so much time on their phones or computers
that they forget to take part in real-life activities around them. This can cause health
problems and lower their school results.

However, technology also provides opportunities for learning and creativity. Students
can access huge amounts of information instantly, collaborate with students
around the world, and develop valuable digital skills. The key is learning to control
technology rather than letting it control you.
