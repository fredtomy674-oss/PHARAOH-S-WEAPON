[2] استخدام (if ~ elif ~ else) يجعل من الممكن تحديد شروط متعددة بالتتابع.

if [Conditional expression 1]:
] [Process when conditional expression 1 is true]
elif [Conditional expression 2]:
] [Process when conditional expression 1 is false and conditional expression 2 is true]
else:
] [Process when both conditional expression 1 and conditional expression 2 are false]
