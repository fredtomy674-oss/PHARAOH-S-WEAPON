Projection of a Line Segment on the Coordinate Axes
To determine the projection of a line segment on either axis, find the
projection of its endpoints on that axis. In each of the following examples,
notice the projection of AB on both the X-axis and Y-axis:
1 AB' is the projection of AB on the X-axis.
2 AB' is the projection of AB on the X-axis.
3 AB' is the projection of AB on the X-axis.
4 AB' is the projection of AB on the Y-axis.
5 Point N is the projection of AB on the Y-axis.
6 AB' is the projection of AB on the Y-axis.

Example 3
Determine the length of the projection of the line segment AB on the X-axis,
where the coordinates of points A and B are A (-3, 4) and B (2, 2).
Draw the line segment AB on the
coordinate plane.
From both points A and B, draw
lines perpendicular to the X-axis,
as shown in the figure.
Thus, point A' (-3, 0) is the projection
of point A on the X-axis, and point
B' (2, 0) is the projection of point B on the X-axis.
So, line segment A'B' is the projection of the line segment AB on the
X-axis.
A'B' = |2| + |-3| = 5.
Therefore, the length of A'B' is 5 length units.
