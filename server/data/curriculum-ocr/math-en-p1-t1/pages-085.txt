Transversal
A transversal is a straight line that intersects
two or more straight lines.
If a straight line intersects two straight lines,
eight angles are formed:
• Four angles are called interior angles,
which lie between the two
straight lines.
• The other four angles are
called exterior angles,
which lie outside the two
straight lines.
