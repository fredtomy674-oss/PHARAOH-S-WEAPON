Sum of Measures of Interior Angles of a Triangle
Straight lines $m$ and $n$ are parallel, straight line $k$ intersects them at $A$ and $B$, and straight line $p$ intersects them at $A$ and $C$.
$\angle 4$, $\angle 1$ and $\angle 5$ form a straight angle
$\therefore m(\angle 4) + m(\angle 1) + m(\angle 5) = 180^\circ$
$\therefore m(\angle 4) = m(\angle 2)$ Alternating Angles
$\therefore m(\angle 5) = m(\angle 3)$ Alternating Angles
$\therefore m(\angle 2) + m(\angle 1) + m(\angle 3) = 180^\circ$

Rule:
The sum of the measures of the interior angles of any triangle is $180^\circ$.
