If a straight line intersects two parallel straight lines, then each two
interior angles on the same side of the transversal are supplementary.

∠ 3 and ∠ 5 are interior angles on the same
side of the transversal

∠ 4 and ∠ 6 are interior angles on the same
side of the transversal
m ( ∠ 3) + m ( ∠ 5) = 180°
m ( ∠ 4) + m ( ∠ 6) = 180°

Example 1
In the opposite figure, there are three
angles, the measure of each is 120°.
Identify these angles and state the reason.
Given that n₁ // n₂ and m is the transversal.

m ( ∠ 3) = 120° (Vertically opposite angles)
m ( ∠ 4) = 120° (Corresponding angles)
m ( ∠ 7) = 120° (Alternating exterior angles)
i.e., the three angles are: ∠ 3 , ∠ 4 and ∠ 7

Example 2
In the opposite figure:
AB // DC . What is the value of X?

AB // DC , ∠ BAD and ∠ ADC are interior
angles on the same side of the transversal, thus:
m ( ∠ BAD) + m ( ∠ ADC) = 180°
3 X + 15° + 75° = 180°
3 X + 90° = 180°
3 X = 180° - 90°
3 X = 90°
X = 90° / 3 = 30°

Verifying the Solution
Substitute X with 30° in the
expression (3 X + 15)°
3 X + 15° = 3 × 30° + 15°
= 90° + 15°
= 105°
m ( ∠ BAD) + m ( ∠ ADC)
= 105° + 75° = 180°
The solution is correct
because the two angles are
supplementary.
