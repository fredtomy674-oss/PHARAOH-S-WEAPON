Relation between Angles
1 Adjacent Angles: They are two angles that lie in the same plane,
share the same vertex, have a common side, and their outer sides
lie on different sides of the common side.
Example:
∠ BAC and ∠ CAD are adjacent angles, because:
• They share the same vertex A, and have
a common side AC.
• The outer sides AD and AB lie on
different sides of the common side AC.

2 Complementary Angles: They are two angles whose measures
have a sum of 90°.
Example:
If m (∠ 1) = 60°, and m (∠ 2) = 30°
Then ∠ 1 and ∠ 2 are complementary angles, because:
m (∠ 1) + m (∠ 2) = 60° + 30° = 90°

3 Supplementary Angles: They are two angles whose measures
have a sum of 180°.
Example:
If m (∠ 1) = 60°, and m (∠ 2) = 120°
Then ∠ 1 and ∠ 2 are supplementary angles, because:
m (∠ 1) + m (∠ 2) = 60° + 120° = 180°

■ Notation in Mathematics
The measure of an angle is
denoted by the letter (m).
Thus, the measure of angle
(ABC) is written as follows:
m (∠ABC).
