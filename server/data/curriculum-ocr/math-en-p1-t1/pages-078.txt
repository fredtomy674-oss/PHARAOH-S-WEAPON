1 Adjacent Angles: They are two angles that lie in the same plane,
share the same vertex, have a common side, and their outer sides
lie on different sides of the common side.
Example:
$\angle$ BAC and $\angle$ CAD are adjacent angles, because:
• They share the same vertex A, and have
a common side $\overrightarrow{AC}$
• The outer sides $\overrightarrow{AD}$ and $\overrightarrow{AB}$ lie on
different sides of the common side $\overrightarrow{AC}$.

■ Notation in Mathematics
The measure of an angle is
denoted by the letter (m).
Thus, the measure of angle
(ABC) is written as follows:
$m$ ($\angle$ABC).

2 Complementary Angles: They are two angles whose measures
have a sum of $90^\circ$.

Example:
If $m$ ($\angle$ 1) = $60^\circ$, and $m$ ($\angle$ 2) = $30^\circ$
Then $\angle$ 1 and $\angle$ 2 are complementary angles, because:
$m$ ($\angle$ 1) + $m$ ($\angle$ 2) = $60^\circ$ + $30^\circ$ = $90^\circ$
