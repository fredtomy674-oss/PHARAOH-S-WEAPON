First Measuring Conceptual Understanding

1 What is the mathematical expression for subtracting (-2) from X?
(a) X - 2
(b) 2 - X
(c) - 2 - X
(d) X + 2

(b) A = l + h

(a) n < 180
(b) n > 180
(c) n <= 180
(d) n >= 180

5 Which of the following pairs of terms are like terms?
(a) 2X, -2X^2

(c) 7X, 7
(d) X^2, Y^2

6 Which of the following is equal to 5a?
(a) 3 + 2a
(b) 2 + 3a
(c) 2a + 3a
(d) 5 + a
