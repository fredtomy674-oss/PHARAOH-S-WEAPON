First Measuring Conceptual Understanding
► Choose the correct answer from the given ones:
1 What is the mathematical expression for subtracting (-2) from X?
(a) X - 2
(b) 2 - X
(c) -2 - X
(d) X + 2

(a) A = 1/2 l h
(b) A = l + h
(c) A = l h
(d) A = l / h

3 What is the suitable equation to find the side length of an equilateral triangle whose perimeter is 12 cm?
(a) X + 3 = 12
(b) 3 X = 12
(c) 2 X = 12
(d) X = 12

(a) n < 180
(b) n > 180
(c) n ≤ 180
(d) n ≥ 180

5 Which of the following pairs of terms are like terms?
(a) 2 X, -2 X²
(b) 3 a, 8 a
(c) 7 X, 7
(d) X², Y²

6 Which of the following is equal to 5 a?
(a) 3 + 2 a
(b) 2 + 3 a
(c) 2 a + 3 a
(d) 5 + a
