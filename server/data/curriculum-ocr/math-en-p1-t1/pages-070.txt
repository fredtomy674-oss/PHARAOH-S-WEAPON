Example 5
Social Life: The table below shows the number of children and youth
members of one club according to their ages. Represent the results using
a pie chart.

| Intervals (Age-group) | 1 - | 11 - | 21 - | 31 - |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Members (Frequency) | 900 | 1,200 | 2,700 | 2,400 |

Since the sum of the measures of central angles in a circle is 360°
the sum of frequencies = 900 + 1,200 + 2,700 + 2,400 = 7,200,
measure of a central angle = $\frac{\text{frequency}}{\text{sum of frequencies}} \times 360^\circ$
► Calculate the measure of the central angle representing each interval.

"Interval 1 -" $\frac{900}{7,200} \times 360^\circ = 45^\circ$
"Interval 11 -" $\frac{1,200}{7,200} \times 360^\circ = 60^\circ$
"Interval 21 -" $\frac{2,700}{7,200} \times 360^\circ = 135^\circ$
"Interval 31 -" $\frac{2,400}{7,200} \times 360^\circ = 120^\circ$
