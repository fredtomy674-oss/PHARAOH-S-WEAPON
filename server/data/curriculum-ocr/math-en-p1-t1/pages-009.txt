Learn!
Proportion Concept: A proportion is an equality of at least two
ratios or two rates.

Examples:
Equality of two ratios
(proportion)
$\frac{2}{3} = \frac{4}{6}$

Equality of two rates
(proportion)
$\frac{80\text{ km}}{1\text{ hour}} = \frac{160\text{ km}}{2\text{ hours}}$

How to write a proportion
Assume the following proportion $\frac{a}{b} = \frac{c}{d}$

Then the quantities $a$, $b$, $c$ and $d$ respectively
are called proportional quantities.

The proportion can be expressed as follows:
$a : b = c : d$
$\lceil\text{ Extremes }\rceil$
$\lfloor\text{ Means }\rfloor$

$a$ and $d$ are called extremes, and $b$ and $c$ are called means.

■ Quick Revision
• A ratio is one of the
methods used to compare
between two quantities of
the same kind.
• The ratio between the
quantities $a$ and $b$ can be
expressed by any of the
following methods: $a$ to $b$,
$a : b$ or $\frac{a}{b}$
