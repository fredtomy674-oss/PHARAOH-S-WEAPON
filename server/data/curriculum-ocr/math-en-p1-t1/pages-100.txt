3 Rectangle
A rectangle is a parallelogram that has one of its angles is a right angle.

▸ A rectangle has all the properties of a parallelogram, in addition to:
1 All its angles are right angles.
2 Its diagonals are equal in length.

4 Rhombus
A rhombus is a parallelogram in which two adjacent sides are equal in length.

▪ Enrichment Information
The Great Pyramid of Giza (Khufu Pyramid)
The Great Pyramid of Giza, is one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World, located in Giza. Its base is a square of side length 230.4 meters, and its current height is 138.8 meters, after it was 146.5 meters high originally. Find out why.

▸ A rhombus has all the properties of a parallelogram, in addition to:
1 All its sides are equal in length.
2 Its diagonals are perpendicular and bisect its interior angles.

5 Square
A square is a parallelogram that has one right angle and two adjacent sides are equal in length.

▸ A square has all the properties of a parallelogram, in addition to:
1 All its sides are equal in length.
2 All its angles are right angles.

3 Its diagonals are equal in length, perpendicular, and bisect its interior angles.
