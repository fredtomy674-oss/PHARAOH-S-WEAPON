$X+(X-12^{\circ})=90^{\circ}$
$2X-12^{\circ}=90^{\circ}$
$2X=90^{\circ}+12^{\circ}=102^{\circ}$
$X=\frac{102^{\circ}}{2}=51^{\circ}$

$(90^{\circ}-X)-X=12^{\circ}$
$90^{\circ}-2X=12^{\circ}$

$X=\frac{78^{\circ}}{2}=39^{\circ}$
