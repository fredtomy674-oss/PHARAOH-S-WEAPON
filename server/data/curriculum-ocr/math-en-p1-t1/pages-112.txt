Example 2

If point $A(4\ k+4\ ,\ -k+3)$ lies on the $Y$-axis, determine the quadrant in
which point $B(-2\ k,\ 4\ k+1)$ is located.

$\because\ A(4\ k+4\ ,\ -k+3)$ lies on the $Y$-axis
$\therefore$ The $x$-coordinate is equal to $0$
$\therefore\ 4\ k+4=0\ \ \therefore\ 4\ k=-4\ \ \ \ \therefore\ k=-1$
By substituting the value of $k$ in point $B$,
the coordinates of $B$ are $B(2\ ,\ -3)$
$\therefore\ B$ is located in the $4^{\text{th}}$ quadrant.
