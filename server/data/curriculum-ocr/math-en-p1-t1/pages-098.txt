$\because \overline{AB} \parallel \overline{DC}$, $\overleftrightarrow{BC}$ is a transversal.
$\therefore \mathrm{m}(\angle B) + \mathrm{m}(\angle C) = 180^\circ$ (Interior angles on the same side of the transversal)
$\therefore 4X + 112^\circ = 180^\circ$
$\therefore 4X = 68^\circ$
$\therefore X = 17^\circ$
$\therefore \mathrm{m}(\angle A) = 3X = 3 \times 17^\circ = 51^\circ$
$\therefore \mathrm{m}(\angle D) = 360^\circ - (112^\circ + 68^\circ + 51^\circ) = 129^\circ$
