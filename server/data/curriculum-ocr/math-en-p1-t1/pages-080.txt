Angle Bisector
It is a ray that divides an
angle into two congruent
angles (equal in measure).

$m(\angle NOZ) + 90^\circ + 50^\circ = 180^\circ$
$m(\angle NOZ) = 180^\circ - 90^\circ - 50^\circ = 40^\circ$
$m(\angle NOM) = m(\angle MOZ) = \frac{40^\circ}{2} = 20^\circ$
$m(\angle MOX) = 20^\circ + 90^\circ + 50^\circ = 160^\circ$


4 Vertically Opposite Angles:
The two vertically opposite angles
are non-adjacent angles formed by
two intersecting straight lines.

• $\angle AMC$ and $\angle BMD$ are vertically opposite angles.
• $\angle AMD$ and $\angle BMC$ are vertically opposite angles.

The two vertically opposite angles are congruent (equal in measure).
In other words:
$m(\angle AMC) = m(\angle BMD)$ and $m(\angle AMD) = m(\angle BMC)$
