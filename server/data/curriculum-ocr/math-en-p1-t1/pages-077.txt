Types of Angles
1 Zero Angle
An angle whose
measure is 0° and
whose sides coincide.
4 Obtuse Angle
An angle whose
measure is more than
90° and less than 180°.
2 Acute Angle
An angle whose
measure is more than 0°
and less than 90°.
5 Straight Angle
An angle whose measure
is 180°, its sides are on
opposite directions and
form a straight line.
3 Right Angle
An angle whose
measure is 90°.
6 Reflex Angle
An angle whose
measure is more than
180° and less than 360°.

Example 1
Find the value of X in each of the following:
1
(2x-10)°
Straight Angle
2
(2x-10)°
150°

1
2 X - 10° = 180°
2 X = 180° + 10°
2 X = 190°
X = \frac{190°}{2}
X = 95°

2
2 X - 10° = 360° - 150°
2 X - 10° = 210°
2 X = 210° + 10°
2 X = 220°
X = \frac{220°}{2}
X = 110°

■ Verify the Solution Yourself:
In problem 1:
Substitute X with 95°
2 X - 10° = 2 \times 95° - 10° = 190° - 10° = 180°
This means that the solution is correct, since the angle is straight.
