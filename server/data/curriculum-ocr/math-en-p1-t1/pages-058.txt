■ Beware of Misleading
Graphs

Carefully read graphs and
understand what they
represent to avoid being
misled by their depiction.

Misleading Graphs

Graphs can be an effective tool for illustrating data; however, sometimes
they can be misleading if not used properly or if they do not display data
correctly, which can adversely impact deductions.

Here are some common ways in which graphs can be misleading:

1 If the vertical axis does not start with zero.

2 If an unequal scale is used on the vertical axis.
