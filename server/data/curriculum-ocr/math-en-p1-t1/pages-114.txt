Example (4)
Determine the coordinates of the midpoint of AB, where the coordinates of
points A and B are A (2, -2) and B (-6, 8).

Let that the midpoint of AB be M
M = (x1 + x2 / 2, y1 + y2 / 2) = (2 + (-6) / 2, -2 + 8 / 2) = (-4 / 2, 6 / 2) = (-2, 3)

Example (5)
If the point M (0, -3) is in the middle of the distance between points
A (X, -10) and B (7, Y), calculate the values of X and Y.

(0, -3) = (7 + X / 2, y - 10 / 2)
7 + X / 2 = 0 ∴ 7 + X = 0 ∴ X = -7
y - 10 / 2 = -3 ∴ y - 10 = -6 ∴ y = 4

Example (6)
If ABCD is a parallelogram where A (-1, 1), B (4, 2), and D (1, 4), determine
the coordinates of the following:
1 The point of intersection of its diagonals.
2 The vertex C.

Let that M be the point of intersection of the diagonals
∴ M is the midpoint of BD ∴ M = (4 + 1 / 2, 2 + 4 / 2) = (2.5, 3)
∴ M is also the midpoint of AC. Let C = (X1, y1)
(-1 + X1 / 2, 1 + y1 / 2) = (2.5, 3)
-1 + X1 / 2 = 2.5 ∴ -1 + X1 = 5 ∴ X1 = 6
1 + y1 / 2 = 3 ∴ 1 + y1 = 6 ∴ y1 = 5 ∴ C = (6, 5)
