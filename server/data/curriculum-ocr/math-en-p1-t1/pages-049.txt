following equations:

(2) $2(X+3)=3(1-X)$

1 $2(X-3)=8$
$2X-6=8$
(Distributive property)
$2X-6+6=8+6$
(Add 6 to both sides)
$2X=14$
(Simplify the equation)
$\frac{2X}{2}=\frac{14}{2}$
(Divid both sides by 2)
$X = 7$ , $7\in Q$
therefore, the solution set = $\{7\}$

2 $2(X+3)=3(1-X)$
$2X+6=3-3X$
(Distributive property)
$2X+3X=3-6$
(Associating the like terms)
$5X=-3$
(Simplify each side)
$X=-\frac{3}{5}$ , $-\frac{3}{5}\in Q$
(Find the value of $X$)
therefore, the solution set = $\{-\frac{3}{5}\}$

Example 5
Culture: Four persons purchased tickets
to the Egyptian Museum in Cairo. They
also purchased souvenirs for $500\text{ LE}$, if
the total cost is $620\text{ LE}$, write the equation
expressing this situation.
What is the price of one ticket?

Assume the price of one ticket is $X\text{ LE}$,
therefore, the price of four tickets is $4X\text{ LE}$
$4X+500=620$
$4X=620-500$
$4X=120$
$X=\frac{120}{4}$
$X=30$
Thus, the price of one ticket is $30\text{ LE}$
