Axes of symmetry in Polygons

Axis of Symmetry:
The axis of symmetry of a shape is a straight
line that divides the shape into two identical
parts. When the shape is folded along the axis
of symmetry, both parts coincide. A shape may
have one or more axes of symmetry, or no axes
of symmetry.

Axes of Symmetry of a Regular Polygon:
The axis of symmetry of a regular polygon is a straight line that passes
through the center of the polygon and divides it into two identical polygons.
