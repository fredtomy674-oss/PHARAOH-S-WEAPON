Algebraic Terms
• Algebraic Term:
An algebraic term is the product (or quotient) of a non-zero number
and at least one variable. The number is called the coefficient of the
algebraic term.

• Like Algebraic Terms:
They are terms with the same variables and raised to the same
powers even though their coefficients are different.
• -3X² and 4X² are like terms.
• 4y and y² are unlike terms because the variable y in both terms
is raised to a different power.
• 2n²m and 2nm² are unlike terms because the variables m and n
in both terms are raised to different powers.
