Concept of Angle: an angle is the union of two rays with the same
initial point.
• The initial point of the two rays is called the vertex of the angle.
• Each one of the two rays is called side of the angle.

Example :
In the opposite figure:
BA ∪ BC = ∠ ABC
∠ ABC can also be referred to
as ∠ CBA, ∠ B or ∠ 1

Units of Measurement for Angles:
Units of measurement for angles are degrees, minutes, and seconds,
such that:
• A degree is equal to 60 minutes (1° = 60').
• A minute is equal to 60 seconds (1' = 60").
