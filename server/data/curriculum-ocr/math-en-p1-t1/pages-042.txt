Properties of Operations

1. Commutative property
In addition:
$a+b=b+a$
In multiplication:
$a \times b=b \times a$

2. Associative property
In addition:
$(a+b)+c=a+(b+c)$
In multiplication:
$(a \times b) \times c=a \times(b \times c)$

3. Distributive property
In addition:
$a(b+c)=a(b)+a(c)$
In subtraction:
$a(b-c)=a(b)-a(c)$

Simplification of Algebraic Expression
- An algebraic expression is in its simplest form when it does not have
any like terms.
- To write an algebraic expression in its simplest form, add the like
terms together after removing the brackets (if exist) using the
distributive property.

Example (5)
Simplify each of the following algebraic expressions:
1. $-X+2 y-8 y+5 X+7$
2. $2(3 X-4)-3(X-2)$

1. $-X+2 y-8 y+5 X+7$
$=-X+5 X+2 y-8 y+7 \quad$ (Commutative property)
$=(-X+5 X)+(2 y-8 y)+7 \quad$ (Associative property)
$=(-1+5) X+(2-8) y+7 \quad$ (Add the coefficients of the like terms)
$=4 X-6 y+7 \quad$ (Write the expression in the simplest form)

2. $2(3 X-4)-3(X-2)$
$=6 X-8-3 X+6 \quad$ (Use the distributive property in expanding the brackets)
$=6 X-3 X-8+6 \quad$ (Commutative property)
$=(6 X-3 X)+(-8+6) \quad$ (Associative property)
$=3 X-2 \quad$ (Write the expression in the simplest form)
