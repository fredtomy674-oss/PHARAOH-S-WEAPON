Unit One Activity

▶ Activity Aim: To employ Artificial Intelligence (AI) capabilities in understanding
scale drawing and to create a cover or background for a Maths book.

▶ Implementation Steps: Work in pairs and try to complete the following steps:
1 Investigate a range of AI applications related to image editing.

2 Find an image suitable for a Maths book cover.

3 Use one of the chosen AI applications to magnify the selected
image at two different scale drawings.

4 Print the resulting images at both scales.
Choose one as your final cover and justify your decision.

5 Write a report that discusses the importance of AI applications in the learning process.
