The Exterior Angle of a Triangle
If ABC is a triangle, D ∈ AB,
D ∉ AB, then ∠ 4 is called an
exterior angle of the triangle ABC,
∠ 1, ∠ 2, and ∠ 3 are the interior
angles of the triangle.

Rule:
The measure of the exterior angle of any triangle is equal to the sum
of the measures of the two non-adjacent interior angles.
