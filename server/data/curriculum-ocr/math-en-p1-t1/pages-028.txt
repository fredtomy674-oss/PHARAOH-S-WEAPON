Second Subtraction of Integers
The subtraction of b from a is the same as the addition of a to the
additive inverse of b.
a-b = a + (-b)

Properties of Multiplication of Integers
Let a, b and c be three integers, the following properties hold for
multiplication:
1 Closure: The product of any two integers is an integer (axbe Z).
2 Commutative: axb = bxa
3 Associative: (a x b) x c = ax (b x c)
4 Existence of a multiplicative identity, which is 1: ax1 = 1x a = a
5 Distributing multiplication on addition and subtraction:
ax (b+c) = axb+axc, ax (b-c) = axb-axc

Note that
When multiplying two
integers with the same sign,
the product is positive.
When multiplying two
integers with opposite signs,
the product is negative.
When multiplying any
number by zero, the product
is zero.
