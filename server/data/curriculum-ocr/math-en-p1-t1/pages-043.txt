Addition of Algebraic Expressions

Example 6
A person has distributed an amount of money among three other persons.
The first person has received $(X + 5)$ LE, the second person has received
$(2X + 3)$ LE, and the third person has received $(3X - 1)$ LE.
Express in the simplest form the total amount of money has been distributed.
If $X = 20$, what is the value of this amount?

The amount distributed by that person can be expressed as follows:
$X + 5 + 2X + 3 + 3X - 1 = X + 2X + 3X + 5 + 3 - 1$
$= (1 + 2 + 3)X + (5 + 3 - 1)$
$= 6X + 7$

At $X = 20$, the amount of money is:
$6 \times 20 + 7 = 120 + 7 = 127\text{ LE}$.

Subtraction of Algebraic Expressions

Example 7
The price of a children's car model is $5X\text{ LE}$.
A shopkeeper purchases it with a discount of
$10\text{ LE}$ and assembles it, then he sells it for
$(6X + 7)\text{ LE}$. Write the algebraic expression
for the shopkeeper's profit.
If $X = 40$, what is the profit?

- The purchase price is $5X - 10$
- The sale price is $6X + 7$
- The profit is calculated as the sale price minus the purchase price
$(6X + 7) - (5X - 10) = 6X + 7 - 5X + 10 = 6X - 5X + 7 + 10$
$= (6 - 5)X + 7 + 10 = X + 17$

Thus, the shopkeeper's profit is $(X + 17)\text{ LE}$
At $X = 40$, the profit is $40 + 17 = 57\text{ LE}$
