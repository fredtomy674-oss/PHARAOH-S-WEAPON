$m(\angle 1) + m(\angle 2) + m(\angle 3) + m(\angle 4) + m(\angle 5) + m(\angle 6) = 360^\circ$

Thus, $m(\angle A) + m(\angle B) + m(\angle C) + m(\angle D) = 360^\circ$
i.e., the sum of the measures of the interior angles of the quadrilateral ABCD is $360^\circ$.

Rule:
The sum of the measures of the interior angles of any quadrilateral is $360^\circ$.
