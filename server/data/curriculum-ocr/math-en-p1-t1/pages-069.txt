Example ④
Nutrition: The opposite pie chart shows the
percentage of calories Sally had in her meals
on one day.
If Sally had 2,500 calories that day, how many
calories did she have at dinner? Find the
measure of the central angle for the breakfast
sector.

Dinner Calories: $2,500 \times 32 \% = 2,500 \times 0.32 = 800$
Measure of the central angle for the breakfast sector :
$\frac{18}{100} \times 360^{\circ} = 64.8^{\circ} \approx 65^{\circ}$
